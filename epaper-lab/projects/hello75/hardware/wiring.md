# hello75 wiring

- Board: 7.5-inch e-paper
- MCU: ESP32 / ESP32-S3

## Pin map

| Signal | MCU Pin | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VCC | 3.3V | Power |
| GND | GND | Ground |
| MOSI | GPIO 11 | Data (DIN) |
| CLK | GPIO 12 | Clock (SCK) |
| CS | GPIO 10 | Chip Select |
| DC | GPIO 13 | Data/Command |
| RST | GPIO 14 | Reset |
| BUSY | GPIO 21 | Busy |
