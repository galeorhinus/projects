## Pattern Notes

- Many uses are not wrong individually. The issue is manuscript-level repetition: “X matters because...” becomes a visible explanatory tic.
- Preferred fix: merge the consequence into the next sentence, so the replacement does not create another short hinge sentence.
- Strong replacement patterns:
  - consequence: “That result shows...”
  - placement: “At this scale...”
  - category: “The category shifts when...”
  - evidence: “The evidence does its work when...”
  - precision: “The distinction changes the claim...”
