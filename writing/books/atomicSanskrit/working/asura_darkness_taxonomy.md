# Asura Darkness Taxonomy — Author's Canonical Spine

**Status:** Canonical taxonomy provided by the author 2026-06-10. Serves as the SPINE for AP10 §3 *The Asuric Mind*. Lesser-known asuras the author has not enumerated should be added as exemplars within the existing categories, not as new top-level categories.

**Frame.** Twenty-four named asuras organized by psychological darkness — pettiness, ego, ignorance, lust, cruelty, materialism. The dial: *interior smallness*, NOT power or strategy. Dark-triad vocabulary (narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy) anchors the categorization at the personal-mind level the author wants.

---

## I. Megalomaniacs and Narcissists

1. **Hiraṇyakaśipu** (हिरण्यकशिपु) — **Narcissism and Megalomania**. Banned worship of any deity, demanded sole worship as God. Could not tolerate his own son's devotion to Viṣṇu. Pathological need for control and validation.
2. **Mahiṣāsura** (महिषासुर) — **Hubris and Chauvinism**. Believed himself invincible by a boon that no *man* could kill him; dismissed women as a threat. Destroyed by Durgā.
3. **Bāṇāsura** (बाणासुर) — **Pride and Possessiveness**. Thousand-armed; consumed by vanity over physical strength; toxic possessiveness over his daughter Uṣā.
4. **Tārakāsura** (तारकासुर) — **Thirst for Supremacy**. Severe penance solely for the power to usurp the heavens. Pure cosmic-political domination motive.

## II. The Greedy and the Hoarders

5. **Hiraṇyākṣa** (हिरण्याक्ष) — **Unchecked Greed and Exploitation**. Dragged the Earth (Bhū-devī) to the bottom of the cosmic ocean. Environmental exploitation; hoarding of cosmic resources.
6. **Vṛtra** (वृत्र) — **Obstruction and Withholding**. Massive serpent who hoarded all waters; caused devastating drought. Malicious control of life-giving resources to exert power.
7. **Dhenukāsura** (धेनुकासुर) — **Territoriality and Selfishness**. Vicious donkey form; aggressively guarded a forest of fruit-bearing trees, refusing all access. Hoarding-as-denying-sustenance.

## III. The Machiavellian and Paranoid

8. **Kaṃsa** (कंस) — **Paranoia and Machiavellianism**. Prophecy that his sister's 8th child would kill him; imprisoned her, murdered her newborns systematically. Ruthless calculated preservation of self at any cost.
9. **Svarbhānu / Rāhu-Ketu** (स्वर्भानु / राहु-केतु) — **Envy and Deceit**. Driven by jealousy of the Devas; used deception and disguise to drink Amṛta. Stealing what one has not rightfully earned.
10. **Pūtanā** (पूतना) — **Deceit and Treachery**. Disguised as beautiful maternal figure to feed poisoned milk to infant Kṛṣṇa. Psychopathic willingness to use trust and innocence as weapons.

## IV. The Slaves to Lust and Base Desires

11. **Jalandhara** (जलन्धर) — **Jealousy and Unchecked Lust**. Born from Śiva's third eye; burning jealousy of Śiva + obsessive lust to possess Pārvatī.
12. **Bhasmāsura** (भस्मासुर) — **Short-sightedness and Lust**. Boon to incinerate by touching head. Blinded by lust for Pārvatī; tricked by Mohinī into touching his own head. Self-destructive unchecked desire.
13. **Andhakāsura** (अन्धकासुर) — **Blind Ignorance**. Physically blind; real darkness was spiritual blindness; lust-driven attempt to abduct his own (foster) mother Pārvatī. Absolute darkest depths of ignorant desire.
14. **Śumbha** (शुम्भ) — **Entitlement and Objectification**. As ruler of the three worlds, believed himself entitled to possess everything beautiful. Ultimate objectification.
15. **Niśumbha** (निशुम्भ) — **Blind Ego and Stubbornness**. Shared arrogance with brother; even while losing to the Goddess, ego refused surrender.

## V. The Cruel and Psychopathic

16. **Narakāsura / Bhaumāsura** (नरकासुर / भौमासुर) — **Sadism and Disrespect**. Hoarded wealth; stole Aditī's earrings; imprisoned 16,000 women. Pure psychopathy; absolute degradation of others for personal pleasure.
17. **Bakāsura** (बकासुर) — **Gluttony and Parasitism**. Giant crane-demon; forced villages to send cartloads of food + human sacrifices. Parasitic exploitation of the weak.
18. **Aghāsura** (अघासुर) — **Spite and Vengeance**. Disguised as giant beautiful serpent with mouth open like a cave, purely to lure and swallow Kṛṣṇa and his friends out of malicious spite.
19. **Kālakeyas** (कालकेय) — **Brutality and Mob Violence**. Fierce clan known for extreme cruelty and bloodlust. Collective violence; joy of inflicting suffering.
20. **Raktabīja** (रक्तबीज) — **Relentlessness of Evil**. Boon: every drop of blood that hit the ground spawned a clone. Proliferating, stubborn, highly adaptive malignant ego refusing to die.

## VI. The Materialists and the Ignorant

21. **Tripurāsuras** (त्रिपुरासुर) — **Tārakākṣa, Kamalākṣa, Vidyunmālī** — **Materialistic Arrogance**. Built three magnificent flying cities of gold, silver, iron. Hubristic belief that advanced technology and material wealth made them superior to divine law.
22. **Virocana** (विरोचन) — **Superficiality**. When Prajāpati taught the "Self," Virocana misinterpreted it as the physical body. Returned to the asuras teaching pure hedonism. Spiritual ignorance + materialism.
23. **Madhu** (मधु) — **Primal Chaos (Tamas)**. Born from Viṣṇu's earwax; unrefined, unthinking darkness of chaos; urge to destroy without higher purpose.
24. **Kaiṭabha** (कैटभ) — **Blind Aggression**. Joined brother Madhu in attacking Brahmā. Foundational brute-force ignorance before the light of consciousness and order.
