## The move

The Epilogue's forward-looking close converts from subdued ("what recognition makes possible" register, the three standing shadows) to a **rallying cry**: the churning. Two centuries of philological labor — the cognate sets, the sound-law tables, the etymological dictionaries, the starred-root indices — are a **churned ocean (समुद्रमन्थन, *samudra-manthana*)**. The pyramid churned it expecting its own amṛta: validation of the imaginary ancestor. What surfaced first was **halāhala** — the racial Arya thesis and its twentieth-century poison. But the ocean itself is real. Ch18 §18.10 already holds the hinge: *"The correspondences have not changed. The explanation has."*

**One move transmutes the whole ocean: take each starred form and check it against the Dhātupāṭha.** Every match transfers an entire word-family from the imaginary ledger to the real one. The apparatus built to demote Sanskrit becomes, re-keyed, the largest empirical index of Sanskrit's radiance ever compiled — halāhala to amṛta in a single re-keying.

## The interpretation — LOCKED (author, 2026-07-17)

**The devas keep the amṛta. That is the point of the episode, and the book keeps the episode's own wisdom.**

- **"Giving it to the devas" means making it radiant again.** The etymology does the work: **देवाः (*devāḥ*)** are *the shining ones*, from ⟪दिव्⟫ (Ch18's own analysis). Amṛta to the devas = the nectar returned to the shining side = **Sanskrit's radiance restored**. The Radiance Thesis is the Manthana's own distribution-rule.
- **No sharing across the battle line.** Handing the nectar to the side that wants to destroy you is not nobility; it is naivete. Mohinī's discrimination *is* the dharmic teaching of the episode — the itihāsa already adjudicated this question.
- **The churners are not the pyramid — and their liberation is a separate battle.** The workforce low in the pyramid (the *certified intellectuals*, then and now) often has no choice; loyalty does not change overnight, and may not change until the pyramid itself collapses. That collapse is a different fight (Second Shanti territory). The book neither romanticizes the churners nor couriers amṛta to them while they still pull the pyramid's rope.
- **Reconciliation with "invite the world":** the moral formula (*Convict the pyramid. Kill the invented ancestor. Invite the world.*) stands — the world is invited to **become** the shining side through re-learning (Wave 3), not the pyramid invited to keep drinking while churning against the Sun. The door is open to anyone who drops the rope; the nectar is not delivered across the line. Distribution by transformation, not by charity.

## The Svarbhānu lock (the strongest structural closure)

**Rāhu was born at the Manthana.** Svarbhānu slipped into the deva line at the distribution, drank the stolen sip, was severed — and the severed head became the immortal eclipse-device. The book's antagonist originates *inside this episode*, which means:

1. **The Epilogue's Manthana close returns the eclipse spine to its own origin-story.** The book opens with Svarbhānu piercing the Sun (Preface, RV 5.40.5) and closes at the churning that created him — run, this time, to amṛta instead of to an eclipse.
2. **The stolen sip is the cautionary proof of the LOCKED interpretation.** The one leak of amṛta to the asura side produced the eclipse. Applied: the pyramid drank a stolen sip of Sanskrit's own churned data — Sanskrit was the key that let comparative grammar exist at all (thesis #23: the calibrant by which the family was partially deciphered) — and the sip became **PIE: a severed head made immortal.** The Preface already carries the exact language: *"made immortal precisely because it was never alive enough to die."* PIE **is** the Rāhu-head of the stolen sip. This is not a new claim; it is the Preface's own line, finally attached to its source-episode.
3. **Layering honesty (keep it):** Svarbhānu is the Vedic name (RV 5.40); the Manthana/Mohinī/severed-head narrative is the itihāsa-Purāṇic continuation the book already navigates on the eclipse spine (Rāhu named as the later name). The Epilogue deployment keeps that layering visible in an endnote, not relitigated in body prose.

## The game, concretized (the rallying cry's spine)

- Two lists, side by side: the starred-root inventory of the etymological dictionaries (**⚠ VERIFY count** — Pokorny ≈ 2,000+ entries; LIV verbal roots ≈ 1,000+; cite precisely or not at all) against the **Dhātupāṭha's 2,168 dhātavaḥ**. One list recited, accented, semantically specified for thousands of years; the other starred, unpronounceable, revised every generation.
- **The invitation: run the match.** Every starred form checked against the dhātu inventory; every match moves a word-family from imaginary to real. Ch18's *sthā / div / jan / kṛt / mātṛ* chains are the demonstration rows; the four-behaviors plan's dhātu-tap endowment table is the template.
- **The protocol (from Codex — the honesty valves; merge with the two-lists beat):**
  1. Take a starred reconstruction.
  2. Ask whether a Sanskrit *dhātuḥ*, *śabda*, or molecule explains the form more directly.
  3. Check whether the semantic field is preserved, narrowed, inverted, or split.
  4. Check whether the receiving language shows vivimorphosis rather than ancestry.
  5. Check whether one Sanskrit atom explains multiple branches better than a hypothetical ancestor does.
  6. **Keep the honest cases; discard the weak ones.** Not every starred item will resolve into Sanskrit — the claim is not 100% conversion.
  7. Build the new map: calibrant source, contact radiance, vivimorphosis.
- **The hammer (from Codex — best single line of either plan):** *PIE no longer gets default parenthood. Every starred form becomes a question placed before Sanskrit, not a verdict over Sanskrit.*
- **Wave-3, swastika-mode:** participatory, distributed, many hands, no central office — the "transmitted by many hands / the Atris' work" paragraph already in the Epilogue is the docking point. The game is what the many hands *do*.
