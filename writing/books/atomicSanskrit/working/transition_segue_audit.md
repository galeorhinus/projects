# Transition and Segue Audit

Pass 1 status: audit only. No manuscript edits have been made from this file yet.

Purpose: make the book read as one continuous proof. Chapter closes should be short hinges, not summaries. Part boundaries need more explanation because the reader is crossing from one kind of evidence to another.

Working rule:

- Chapter close: 1-3 sentences. Established fact -> unavoidable next question.
- Part opener: 250-500 words. Name the Part, explain why it is called that, identify what the chapters in the Part prove, and state what the reader should see by the end.
- Chapter opening: should receive the previous hinge cleanly.

## Structural Finding

The book currently has Part titles and subtitles in `as_book.yaml`, but no prose Part openers. The Part breaks therefore look formally clean but do not yet guide the reader across major changes in evidence-mode: polemic -> internal testimony -> sound-field -> atomic architecture -> preservation -> cross-examination -> remedy.

Recommendation for later pass: add short Part opener prose after each `\part{}` break. Implementation can be either:

- add small `as_part_XX_*.md` files and adjust assembly handling, or
- insert the Part opener at the beginning of the first chapter in each Part.

The first option is cleaner structurally. The second option is simpler mechanically.
