## 0. The three moves this plan executes

1. **Swap the chapter's documentary spine from *mother* to *jan / gen*.** *jan* (⟪जन्⟫, to beget / to be born) → *kin, kind, king, kindred, genus, gene, nation, nature, race*. It replaces *mother* wherever *mother* was carrying the **historical / dictionary-shift** load.

The dictionary shift is the *evidence* the bakery went industrial.

## 1. Why *jan* for the *mother* slot (not *kṛt*)

You asked: *kṛt* or *gen*, whichever is stronger. For the slot *mother* occupies — the **opening spark + the dictionary-shift section** — *jan* wins decisively, and *kṛt* is better left where it already does its best work.

| | *jan / gen* | *kṛt* |
|---|---|---|
| 19th-c. citations naming it | **three** — Skeat (Root 119 JAN), Chambers ("from Sans. jan"), Müller ("the Sanskrit root JAN") | one, weaker — Skeat Root 37 KART, "*allied to* Skt. kṛt" |
| Müller — the book's arch-priest — credits it **by name** | **yes** (catch the founder confessing) | no equivalent quote |
| Thematic tie to the book's spine | **the race-word atom** — *kind, race, nation, gene* are the exact words the racial Arya thesis is built from | *cut* — no tie to RAT |
| Best job in the chapter | the **documentary / historical** proof (dictionaries credited it, then buried it) | the **mechanism** proof (s-mobile = misheard *suṭ*; root-extension = *kta*; zero-grade = *ṛ*) |

**Division of labor after the rewrite:**
- ***kṛt* = how the fraud is manufactured** (§18.5 Recipe — the device-by-device dissection; keeps the richest device-set).
- ***jan* = how the fraud entered the record and erased the source** (opening + §18.6 — the dated, named dictionary shift; carries the race theme).

They complement; they do not overlap. §18.5's *jan* rerun stays a single "the recipe reruns" paragraph + the genh/jan figure pair; §18.6 does *jan*'s full documentary heavy-lifting in prose.
