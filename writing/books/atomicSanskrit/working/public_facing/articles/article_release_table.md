## Purpose

This table collects the public-facing article ideas for *Atomic Sanskrit*, with suggested outlet targets, rough timing, and current status. The sequence should create one strong publisher-attention clip before submission, build public awareness before launch, and keep the argument alive after publication.

The newest public frame is now central: Sanskrit is not merely a natural language and not a codified language. It belongs to a third category — *saṃskṛti*: calibrated created order.

## Recommended Short Sequence

1. **Before publisher submission:** Pāṇini article in Swarajya.
2. **Before or soon after publisher submission:** Third-category article.
3. **After publisher interest:** Sonomer article in a mainstream outlet if possible.
4. **Pre-launch:** Fractal-architecture essay or retroflex article.
5. **Pre-launch / launch:** Dhātu article in Swarajya longform.
6. **Launch:** Seven Claims overview.
7. **Post-launch:** PIE, Vedas, ārya, Vedic/Classical category-error, and swastika/pyramid essays.

## Submission Notes

- Do not send the same article to multiple outlets at once if exclusivity is expected.
- Use the sharpest version for aligned outlets.
- Use the most neutral version for mainstream outlets.
- Publish arguments, not chapter excerpts, before a publisher has seen the manuscript.
