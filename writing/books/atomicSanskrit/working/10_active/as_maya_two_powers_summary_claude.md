## 0. The conflation risk — read this first

Two different pairs came up in discussion. They are **not** the same distinction and must not be merged:

| | What the "two" are | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Two *śaktis*** | *āvaraṇa* and *vikṣepa* — two powers **of one** *māyā* | Advaita analysis (*Vedāntasāra* §§51–54). **Not Vedic vocabulary.** |
| **B. Two deployments** | *deva*-side and *asura*-side use of *māyā* | The Ṛgveda itself, 135 occurrences |

A is an *analysis of how māyā operates*. B is *who wields it and to what end*. A deployment plan needs to decide which one it is using, or state explicitly how they compose.

## 1. A — The two *śaktis* (Vedāntasāra)

**Exactly two powers, not three.** (A "third power, *viveka śakti*" is a misremembering: *viveka* is the discrimination that **dispels** *māyā*, not one of its powers — it is the first of the *sādhana-catuṣṭaya*. The plausible source of the error is the *guṇa* correlation, which *is* threefold: *tamas*→*āvaraṇa*, *rajas*→*vikṣepa*, *sattva*→*jñāna śakti*, the revealing power.)

**§51** — *"This ignorance has two powers, viz., the power of concealment and the power of projection."*
Nikhilananda's note: *"Concealment — Because this power conceals the real nature of Brahman… Projection — This power gives rise to the illusion of name and form."*

**§52 — आवरण शक्ति (*āvaraṇa śakti*), concealing.** *"Just as a small patch of cloud, by obstructing the vision of the observer, conceals, as it were, the solar disc extending over many miles, similarly ignorance, though limited by nature, yet obstructing the intellect of the observer, conceals, as it were, the Self which is unlimited."* Quotes **Hastāmalaka 10**.

> Sadānanda's own qualifier is load-bearing for the eclipse spine: *"As it were — The sun, as a matter of fact, can never be covered by a cloud."* The concealment is in the seer's vision. The Sun is untouched.

**§53** — bridge: the Self so covered becomes subject to *saṃsāra*, *"just as a rope may become a snake due to the concealing power of one's own ignorance."*

**§54 — विक्षेप शक्ति (*vikṣepa śakti*), projecting.** *"Just as ignorance regarding a rope, by its inherent power, gives rise to the illusion of a snake etc. in the rope covered by it, so also ignorance, by its own power creates in the Self covered by it, such phenomena as ether etc."* Quotes **Vākyasudhā 13**: *"The power of projection creates all from the subtle bodies to the cosmos."*

Nikhilananda's summary phrase: *"Ignorance endowed with these twin powers of concealment and projection."*

**Note on the rope-snake:** it spans **both** §53 and §54 — concealing in §53, projecting in §54. It is one illustration doing double duty across the pair, not an illustration of *vikṣepa* alone.

**Source.** Swami Nikhilananda, trans., *Vedāntasāra of Sadānanda* (Advaita Ashrama), Devanāgarī text with translation and notes, pp. 39–41 — <https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.238117>

**Verification limit:** quotations above are from that edition's **English translation and notes**, via OCR of a scanned copy. The Devanāgarī did not survive OCR legibly. **The Sanskrit wording of §§51–54 is *not* independently verified here** — a clean text (GRETIL or print) is required before quoting Sanskrit rather than translation.

## 2. B — The two deployments (Ṛgveda, verified)

### Deva-side — *māyā* as measuring, forming, capability

- **RV 3.61.7** — **मही मित्रस्य वरुणस्य माया** *mahī mitrasya varuṇasya māyā*, "great is the *māyā* of Mitra and Varuṇa" — and it sets light in many places.
- **RV 5.85.5** — **आसुरस्य … मायां वरुणस्य** *āsurasya… māyāṃ varuṇasya*: Varuṇa's *māyā*, called ***āsura***, and what it does is **measure** — *yo mame pṛthivīm*, "who measured out the earth with the sun." Atom ⟪मा⟫, *to measure*. (Already used in Ch 3 §3.5.)
- **RV 5.78.6** — the Aśvins' *māyābhiḥ*.

### Indra defeats *māyā* **with** *māyā*

- **RV 1.11.7** — **मायाभिरिन्द्र मायिनं त्वं शुष्णमवातिरः** — "with *māyā*s, O Indra, you struck down the *māyin* Śuṣṇa." (Already the Ch 2 epigraph.)
- **RV 5.30.6** — *pra māyābhir māyinaṃ sakṣad indraḥ*.
- **RV 1.32.4b** — *āt māyinām amināḥ pra uta māyāḥ* — he diminished the *māyā*s of the *māyin*s.

### Asura-side — *māyā* as concealment and enclosure

- **RV 2.11.9b** — **मायाविनम् वृत्रम्** *māyāvinam vṛtram*.
- **RV 1.53.7** — **नमुचिं नाम मायिनम्** *namuciṃ nāma māyinam*.
- **RV 5.40.6a, 5.40.8d** — **स्वर्भानोः … मायाः** *svarbhānoḥ… māyāḥ*, the deceptions that cover the Sun.

### What follows

In the Ṛgveda *māyā* is **capability** — measure, form, craft, contrivance. Its character is fixed by the act described, not by the word. This is the same *anekaśabda* method Ch 3 §3.6 already applies to *asu-ra* / *a-sura*: **the action described in the mantra identifies the word.** Ch 2 §2.1 states this already ("its character depends on purpose"); the corpus count supports it at scale.

## 3. How A and B relate — proposed framing

*Āvaraṇa* and *vikṣepa* **do not occur in the Ṛgveda in that technical sense.** What the Ṛgveda supplies is the *operation*: Svarbhānu covering the Sun (5.40), Vṛtra the *māyāvin* enclosing the waters (2.11.9). The *Vedāntasāra* supplies the explicit analysis of that operation.

That is the relationship the book already argues for grammar:

> **Sadānanda stands to Vedic *māyā* as Pāṇini stands to Vedic grammar** — making explicit what the mantras already do.

**Framing constraint.** The conventional academic account calls this *māyā* "developing" from magic-power to cosmic illusion. That is the chronological framing the book's rules refuse, and the corpus does not require it: two domains, one word, different work. Do not read Advaita's illusion-sense back into the Ṛgveda, where the word plainly does not carry it.

## 4. Why this is worth deploying (for the reviewers to test)

The book currently uses *māyā* almost entirely as **concealment** — Ch 1 §1 ("conceal their motive, disguise deformation"), Ch 2 §2.1 *āsurī māyā*, Ch 3 §3.5, Ch 18 §18.x, Ch 20. All of that is *āvaraṇa*.

But the pyramid does not only hide. It **projects a substitute** — PIE, the family tree, the racial Arya thesis, the botanical account. Ch 3 §3.5's containment arsenal splits the operation four ways (*concealment / blockade / quarantine / enclosure*), and all four are varieties of *āvaraṇa*. **There is currently no term in the arsenal for the manufacture-and-projection half.**

This also sharpens the eclipse spine specifically: an eclipse is **visible-but-darkened**, not hidden. Svarbhānu does not remove the Sun; he puts something in front of it. Concealment **plus** projection is the eclipse.
