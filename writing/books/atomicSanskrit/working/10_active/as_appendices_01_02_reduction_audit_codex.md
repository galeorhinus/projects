## Purpose of This Pass

The main-body reduction pass removed repetition without compressing the surviving explanation. This appendix pass applies the same standard. It does not reduce technical evidence merely because that evidence is detailed. It looks for repeated framing, historical excursions that do not advance the appendix, and prose that restates a table or a preceding section.

The appendices perform work the main body should not:

1. preserve documentary trails;
2. show calculations and comparison tables;
3. provide reproducible research methods;
4. carry extended institutional evidence;
5. state concrete proposals that follow from the book's argument.

Those functions remain protected.

## Ranking

- **A — Retain:** unique evidence, method, calculation, or necessary conclusion.
- **B — Tighten:** necessary argument surrounded by repetition.
- **C — Consolidate:** useful material whose full explanation already appears elsewhere.
- **D — Remove or relocate:** no distinct work in this appendix.

The proposed reductions below remove repeated material rather than shorten every sentence. The retained prose should remain patient, direct, and readable to an intelligent non-specialist.
