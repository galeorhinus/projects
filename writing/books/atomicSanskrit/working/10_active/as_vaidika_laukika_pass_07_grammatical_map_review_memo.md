## Composite Grammatical Map

The appendix now assembles the grammatical differences in one three-column
table:

- grammatical category;
- vaidika deployment;
- laukika deployment.

The rows cover pitch accent, duration, lineage-preserved sound realizations,
contextual articulations, sandhi and hiatus, nominal endings, pronouns, augment
and injunctive, verbal modes and endings, participles, gerunds, infinitives,
*upasarga* placement, particles, sentence accent, compounds, derivation,
composition, and style.

The table describes distribution. It does not treat differences between the
domains as evidence that one form changed chronologically into another.
