### Proposed

Keep the original passage through:

> That is the nectar.

Keep the original research invitation as prose. It already states the method with urgency and ends with two strong lines:

> The rest of the ocean stands there, indexed, waiting. PIE no longer gets default parenthood: every starred form becomes a question placed before Sanskrit, not a verdict over Sanskrit.

4. Compare them with the recorded forms and identify plausible routes of contact.

Do not replace the invitation with the numbered list; the list should make the invitation usable after the original prose has made it desirable.
