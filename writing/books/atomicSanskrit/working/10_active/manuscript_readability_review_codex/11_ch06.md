> Modern linguistics later labeled these phenomena as phonetic erosion, morphological reanalysis, and lexical replacement. Patañjali documented the phenomena, had the vocabulary for them, measured their asymmetry, and placed grammar on the side of the engineered form — thousands of years before any modern philological project.

### Proposed

## C06-003 - Separate analytical documents from recitation checks

**Source:** [§6.5, anti-entropy machinery](/Users/paragtope/projects/writing/books/atomicSanskrit/as_1_06_apabhramsa.md:93)  
**Action:** REPLACE  
**Status:** APPLIED — 2026-07-23  
**Applied:** [as_1_06_apabhramsa.md](/Users/paragtope/projects/writing/books/atomicSanskrit/as_1_06_apabhramsa.md:93) — 2026-07-23  

### Original

> The *Aṣṭādhyāyī* specifies grammatical operations, the *Vārttikāni* analyze and refine their application, and the *Mahābhāṣya* explains and defends the resulting system. Around them, the **षड्वेदाङ्गानि (*ṣaḍ-vedāṅgāni*)** — *Śikṣā*, *Vyākaraṇa*, *Nirukta*, *Kalpa*, *Chandas*, and *Jyotiṣa* — preserve additional dimensions of pronunciation, analysis, meter, and use. The *Prātiśākhya* texts document the phonetic requirements of particular recensions, while the *padapāṭha* separates a continuous line into words and the *krama*, *jaṭā*, and *ghana* recitations recombine those words under increasingly dense constraints. Since each layer checks a different property, a deviation that escapes one layer can still become audible or structurally impossible in another.

### Proposed

Several analytical documents explain different parts of the architecture. The *Aṣṭādhyāyī* specifies grammatical operations, the *Vārttikāni* examine their application, and the *Mahābhāṣya* explains and defends the system. The **षड्वेदाङ्गानि (*ṣaḍ-vedāṅgāni*)** — *Śikṣā*, *Vyākaraṇa*, *Nirukta*, *Kalpa*, *Chandas*, and *Jyotiṣa* — preserve additional knowledge about pronunciation, analysis, meter, procedure, and use. The *Prātiśākhya* texts record the phonetic requirements of particular recensions.

The recitation methods provide another set of checks. The *padapāṭha* separates a continuous line into words, while *krama*, *jaṭā*, and *ghana* recombine those words under increasingly dense constraints. Because each method tests a different property, a deviation that escapes one check may become audible or structurally impossible in another.

## C06-004 - Present the four-Veda functions as this book's scheme

**Status:** OPEN  
### Original

> The differences exist, but Sanskrit's own organization assigns work to them. The four Vedas operate as functional streams: the Ṛgveda invokes and addresses, the Yajurveda joins mantra to measured action, the Sāmaveda transforms mantra through melodic pattern, and the Atharvaveda protects, corrects, heals, and restores balance. Different Rigvedic *maṇḍalas* make metrical and compositional choices suited to different contexts, while the Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka, and Upaniṣad layers preserve different kinds of instruction and inquiry.

### Proposed

The differences exist, but Sanskrit's own organization assigns work to them. This book follows one functional scheme for the four Vedas: the Ṛgveda invokes and addresses; the Yajurveda joins mantra to measured action; the Sāmaveda carries mantra through melodic pattern; and the Atharvaveda protects, corrects, heals, and restores balance.[NOTE: veda-four-functions] This scheme does not exhaust what each Veda contains. It shows why distinct styles can serve distinct work without becoming stages in a chronology. Rigvedic *maṇḍalas* likewise make metrical and compositional choices suited to different settings, while Saṃhitā, Brāhmaṇa, Āraṇyaka, and Upaniṣad preserve different forms of instruction and inquiry.
