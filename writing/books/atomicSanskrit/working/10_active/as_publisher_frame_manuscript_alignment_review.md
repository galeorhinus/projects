## 1. Conclusion

The book already contains most of what you have been telling people. It does not yet arrange those ideas as clearly as your spoken explanation does.

The existing manuscript strongly establishes Sanskrit's engineering, the greater feat of systems engineering, two enemies, distributed custodianship, and the contrast with an apex. It also describes the three śāntis and explicitly assigns every series volume to the second domain.

The missing emphasis is the governing relationship:

> This is a book about Sanātan's inside-out architecture. Sanskrit is the language system through which the reader examines it. The three śāntis introduce the expanding domains; the language demonstrates recurring principles of construction and correction.

**The phrase “inside-out” currently appears in the thesis summary, but not in the manuscript sources.** The fuller claim that the three śāntis form a fractal map is also absent from the body. A reader can reconstruct much of your argument, but the book should state the relationship and then demonstrate it.

This calls for a focused framing revision, not a new book structure. Keep the 21 chapters, seven parts, eclipse sequence, technical demonstrations, and polemical argument. Make the purpose explicit near the beginning, explain the connection at the construction-to-preservation transition, and return to it in the epilogue.
