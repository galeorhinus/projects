# Endnote Verification Batch 003 — Unresolved Apparatus

**Audit date:** 2026-09-01  
**Scope:** The seven endnotes that contained explicit `[VERIFY]` instructions and the three remaining entries whose Short forms or structural fields were unfinished.

## Outcome

All ten notes now have complete Short forms, plural `Deployments` fields, usable locators, and no unresolved verification instruction. Four claims required factual or attribution corrections; six held and received stronger sources, boundaries, or locators. The body changed only where the audit found unsupported wording, a source-specific spelling, or an overstatement about institutional training.
