## Results

| Endnote | Risk | Result | Finding and action |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| `parampara-vyakaranam-bhartrhari-position-1` | P0 | Strengthened | Reactivated for Chapter 18 §18.7 and rewritten around exact primary passages. The note explicitly separates these sources from the book's three-part speculation. |
