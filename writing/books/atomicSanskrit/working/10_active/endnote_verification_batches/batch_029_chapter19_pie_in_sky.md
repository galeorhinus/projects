## Manuscript Proposals and Decisions

Three factual corrections were identified during the batch. The author reviewed them on 2026-09-03:

1. **Applied.** The chronology now follows Schleicher's *Ursprache*, the English term recorded by 1905, and Lehmann's use of both *Proto-Indo-European* and *PIE* in 1952.
2. **Applied.** The body now states the architectural distinction: the inserted *s* belongs to **⟪कृ⟫**, not to the cutting atom **⟪कृत्⟫**. The endnote records the broader corpus result without assigning all 650 normalized **kṛt** tokens to one of the two homonymous atoms.
3. **Applied.** The **युक्त (*yukta*)** locator is now RV 10.102.6.
