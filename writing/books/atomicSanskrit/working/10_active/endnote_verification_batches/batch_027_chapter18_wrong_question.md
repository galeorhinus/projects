## Manuscript Review

The chapter initially remained unchanged while the endnotes were verified. The author then approved a replacement for the paragraph about pyramidal labor systems.

### Proposal 1 — Bactria–Margiana

**Before:** “In Bactria–Margiana, centralized authorities organized corvée labor to build and maintain irrigation works and controlled dependent agricultural workers.”

**After:** The paragraph now describes the central control of water, land, dependent labor, and wealth directly. The qualification that this account is an archaeological reconstruction remains in the endnote rather than interrupting the body.

**Status:** Applied as part of the approved paragraph replacement.

### Proposal 2 — Sintashta

**Before:** “At Sintashta, leaders placed a substantial public-works burden on the population to raise fortifications and burial mounds within a society marked by hierarchy and warfare.”

**After:** Removed.

The cited study estimates at most a few days of work per person each year and expressly says the difference was not enormous. Sintashta therefore diluted a paragraph meant to establish labor extracted by a pyramidal system.

**Status:** Applied as part of the approved paragraph replacement. The source remains archived in the research registry but no longer supports the endnote or body.
