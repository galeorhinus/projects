## Manuscript Review

The chapter initially remained unchanged while the endnotes were verified. The following three corrections were then approved and applied.

### Proposal 1 — Grammar and number

**Before:** “Sanskrit absorbs this distinctive regional sounds and gives it architectural place.”

**After:** “Sanskrit includes these distinctive regional sounds and gives each one a place within its architecture.”

**Status:** Applied.

### Proposal 2 — Mundari subject marking

**Before:** “Mundari weakens the independent doer differently: pronominal subject marking belongs inside the predicate rather than letting a noun-subject stand as the sole sovereign anchor.”

**After:** “Mundari handles the subject differently. A pronoun that identifies the subject can attach to the verb or to the word immediately before it, even when that word is not the subject. The sentence therefore does not depend on a separate noun to identify the doer.”

**Status:** Applied with the author's requested plain-language replacement for *pronominal*.

### Proposal 3 — The role of intellect

**Before:** “Vāc reveals her form to the prepared (*tanvaṃ vi sasre*, RV 10.71.4), and the Self reveals its form to the one it chooses, bypassing the intellect entirely (*vivṛṇute tanūṃ svām*, Kaṭha Upaniṣad 1.2.23 / Muṇḍaka 3.2.3).”

**After:** “Vāc reveals her form to the prepared (*tanvaṃ vi sasre*, RV 10.71.4), and the Self reveals its form to the one it chooses; intellect alone cannot compel that disclosure (*vivṛṇute tanūṃ svām*, Kaṭha Upaniṣad 1.2.23 / Muṇḍaka 3.2.3).”

**Status:** Applied.
