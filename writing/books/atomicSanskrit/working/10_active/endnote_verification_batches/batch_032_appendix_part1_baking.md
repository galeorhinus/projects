## Appendix Corrections — Applied 2026-09-03

### 1. Deccan College chronology

**Current:**

> The institution became *Poona College* in 1851, *Deccan College* in 1864, and the *Deccan College Post-Graduate and Research Institute* after independence.

**Proposed:**

> It became *Poona College* in 1851. The name *Deccan College* dates from the move begun in 1864 and completed when the new campus opened in 1868. It reopened as the *Deccan College Post-Graduate and Research Institute* in 1939.
