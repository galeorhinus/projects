**Audit date:** 2026-09-03

## Results

| Endnote | Risk | Result | Finding and action |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| `smrti-as-mnemoniture` | P1 | Corrected | The older note placed all classical literature and regional retellings inside one “standard *smṛti* corpus.” It now identifies the central textual classes and treats later compositions, performances, and retellings as carriers of the stories rather than forcing them into one narrow canonical list. |
| `flexture-natyashastra-dance` | P1 | Narrowed | Bharata's *Nāṭyaśāstra* documents bodily and hand gesture, while the *Abhinaya Darpaṇa* supplies its own enumeration. The note no longer claims that embodied transmission has the “same engineering rigor” as *śruti*; it states the observable specification and correction mechanism. |
| `shruti-as-auditure` | P0 | Corrected | Exact pronunciation, accent, sequence, and recitation techniques are documented for practicing Vedic traditions. The note no longer implies that every *Brāhmaṇa*, *Āraṇyaka*, and *Upaniṣad* available today has an identical phoneme-level apparatus or all eleven recitation paths. |
| `auditory-temporal-gap-resolution` | P0 | Verified | The 2019 experiment reports a 3.19 ms mean broadband threshold; Moore's review gives a typical 2–3 ms threshold. The body accurately presents the cautious 2–5 ms range and names the tested population. |
| `metered-verbal-pattern-error-detection` | P1 | Verified | The cited experiment directly tested unexpected lyric and melody changes in memorized songs. The note appropriately limits the inference to an additional detection cue and does not claim that singing always improves initial learning. |
| `masoretic-engineered-preservation` | P0 | Corrected | Replaced “four canonical layers” with four features used by the comparison, removed an unsupported universal count of accent signs, and acknowledged manuscript variation before stabilization. The Aleppo Codex record directly supports the text, vocalization, accent, and Masoretic-note architecture. |
| `quranic-engineered-preservation` | P0 | Corrected | Nasser supports the seven- and ten-reading canonizations. The note now describes named teaching chains without claiming that every historical transmitter survives in a complete teacher-by-teacher register. |
| `arabic-religio-political-authority` | P0 | Reconfirmed | Institutional and court sources confirm the 1924 edition, publication permission, unlicensed-circulation prosecution, criminal penalties, and confiscation powers used by the chapter's control case. |
| `latin-vulgate-engineered-preservation` | P0 | Corrected | Removed the claim that every monastic copy was checked against an archetype and separated medieval copying from modern stemmatic reconstruction. Vatican records confirm the authorized editions and the 1979 *Nova Vulgata* promulgation. |
| `tolkappiyam-grammar-and-tamil-change` | P0 | Verified | The three divisions and their scope, later Tamil change, and nineteenth-century return of Sangam works to wider print circulation remain supported. The note already avoids claiming absolute unintelligibility for every modern speaker. |
| `chandas-laghu-guru-virahanka-sequence` | P1 | Reconfirmed | Batch 000 established the laghu/guru recurrence and the Piṅgala–Virahāṅka–Gopāla–Hemacandra sequence. No new issue appears in Chapter 14's deployment. |
| `whole-language-sutra-discipline-comparator` | P1 | Verified as synthesis | This note records the book's comparator and connects the classifications established in Chapters 2 and 6 to the six-part test in Chapter 14. It does not present that synthesis as an external scholarly classification. |
| `petrified-bounded-forms` | P1 | Qualified | The historical control cases support the bounded-form/changing-speech distinction. The note clearly labels *petrification* and *revivification* as this book's terms. |
| `botanical-drift-prestige-memory` | P1 | Verified and qualified | Ferguson, Abd-El-Jawad, Haugen, and the Milroys support institutional prestige and language-standardization pressures. The long-memory consequence remains explicitly identified as the book's architectural inference. |
