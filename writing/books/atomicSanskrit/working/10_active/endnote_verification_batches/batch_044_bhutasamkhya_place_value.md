## Results

| Endnote | Risk | Result | Finding and action |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| `bhutasamkhya-word-place-value` | P0 | Verified | Petrocchi documents the decimal place-value use of words, the units-to-higher-places convention, its metrical and mnemonic purpose, and the specific examples used in the body.
