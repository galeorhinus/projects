### Applied Correction 1 — Opening reference to *Ghanapāṭhī*

**Before:** “The घनपाठ (*ghana-pāṭha*) is mastered by senior reciters who bear a title recognized across Vedic communities.”

**After:** “The घनपाठ (*ghana-pāṭha*) is mastered by senior reciters who bear the title घनपाठी (*Ghanapāṭhī*).”

### Applied Correction 2 — Scope of the named *Śikṣā* texts

**Before:** “The listed शिक्षा (*Śikṣā*) texts are compact because they are training manuals, not survey essays. The पाणिनीयशिक्षा (*Pāṇinīya-Śikṣā*), याज्ञवल्क्यशिक्षा (*Yājñavalkya-Śikṣā*), वासिष्ठीशिक्षा (*Vāsiṣṭhī-Śikṣā*), आपिशलिशिक्षा (*Āpiśali-Śikṣā*), and भारद्वाजशिक्षा (*Bhāradvāja-Śikṣā*) belong to that world of disciplined production.”

**After:** “The शिक्षा (*Śikṣā*) literature records several kinds of preservation instruction. The पाणिनीयशिक्षा (*Pāṇinīya-Śikṣā*), याज्ञवल्क्यशिक्षा (*Yājñavalkya-Śikṣā*), वासिष्ठीशिक्षा (*Vāsiṣṭhī-Śikṣā*), आपिशलिशिक्षा (*Āpiśali-Śikṣā*), and भारद्वाजशिक्षा (*Bhāradvāja-Śikṣā*) belong to that world. Some specify how a reciter should produce and time sounds. The वासिष्ठीशिक्षा (*Vāsiṣṭhī-Śikṣā*) also counts units in its Vedic corpus so that additions or losses can be detected.”

### Applied Correction 3 — Three *prakṛtis*, eight *vikṛtis*

**Before:** “The five प्रकृतिपाठाः (*prakṛti-pāṭhāḥ*) are primary recitations. The six विकृतिपाठाः (*vikṛti-pāṭhāḥ*) are modified recitations that apply deeper permutations.”

**After:** “The three प्रकृतिपाठाः (*prakṛti-pāṭhāḥ*) are primary recitations. The eight विकृतिपाठाः (*vikṛti-pāṭhāḥ*) are modified recitations that apply deeper permutations.”

### Applied Correction 4 — The *Ghana* title and the remaining six *vikṛtis*

**Before:** “A घनपाठी (*Ghanapāṭhī*) has mastered the highest common recitational density and is recognized as such across Vedic communities by title.”

**After:** “A reciter who masters this form bears the title घनपाठी (*Ghanapāṭhī*).”

**Before:** “The six विकृतिपाठाः (*vikṛti-pāṭhāḥ*) add further permutation: माला (*mālā*, garland), शिखा (*śikhā*, peak), रेखा (*rekhā*, line), ध्वज (*dhvaja*, flag), दण्ड (*daṇḍa*, staff), and रथ (*ratha*, chariot).”

**After:** “The other six विकृतिपाठाः (*vikṛti-pāṭhāḥ*) complete the set: माला (*mālā*, garland), शिखा (*śikhā*, peak), रेखा (*rekhā*, line), ध्वज (*dhvaja*, flag), दण्ड (*daṇḍa*, staff), and रथ (*ratha*, chariot).”

The following summary sentence would then read: “The same underlying संहिता (*saṃhitā*) is locked into place eleven ways: continuous flow, separated words, overlapping pairs, the braid of जटा (*jaṭā*), the six additional विकृति (*vikṛti*) patterns, and the density of घन (*ghana*).”

### Applied Correction 5 — Comparative close

**Before:** “None has the same redundancy depth, cross-lineage independence, and living recitational architecture.”

**After:** “Among these comparison cases, only the Vedic system uses connected recitation, word separation, overlapping pairs, reversals, and longer prescribed recombinations of the same passage.”
