## Proposed Manuscript Corrections

All three changes below were approved and applied to Chapter 6 on 2026-09-02.

### 1. Epigraph — quote the *Mahābhāṣya* exactly

**Current:**

> भूयांसोऽपभ्रंशा अल्पीयांसः शब्दाः ।
>
> *bhūyāṃso 'pabhraṃśā alpīyāṃsaḥ śabdāḥ.*

**Proposed:**

> भूयांसोऽपशब्दाः, अल्पीयांसः शब्दाः ।
>
> *bhūyāṃso 'paśabdāḥ, alpīyāṃsaḥ śabdāḥ.*

**Reason:** The current epigraph substitutes *apabhraṃśāḥ* for the source's *apaśabdāḥ* while attributing the line directly to the *Mahābhāṣya*. Section 6.2 already explains the relationship between the two terms, so the exact quotation strengthens the sequence.

### 2. §6.3 — separate Patañjali's list from modern classifications

**Current:**

> Modern linguistics later labeled these movements phonetic erosion, morphological reanalysis, and lexical replacement. Patañjali had already analyzed all three in गौः (*gauḥ*).

**Proposed:**

> Modern linguistics gives different names to the ways a word can change. Patañjali begins from another question: which form does Sanskrit recognize as the word, and which forms have fallen away from it?

**Reason:** Patañjali lists the four forms as *apabhraṃśāḥ*. The passage does not assign the four examples to the three modern categories named in the current sentence.

### 3. §6.6 — correct the Mohenjo-daro skeleton count

**Current:**

> Six skeletons in the most cited cluster became evidence for one massacre and then a race-replacement narrative.

**Proposed:**

> Thirty-seven skeletons found in different areas and without one shared archaeological context were made to tell a single story of massacre and racial replacement.

**Reason:** Dales counts thirty-seven skeletons overall. The most frequently reproduced Room 74 group contained fourteen, while six skeletons belonged to a separate lane group. The proposed sentence keeps the chapter's point without repeating the numerical conflation.
