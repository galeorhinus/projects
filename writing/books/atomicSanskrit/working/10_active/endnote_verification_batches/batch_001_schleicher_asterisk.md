# Endnote Verification Batch 001 — Schleicher and the Reconstruction Asterisk

**Audit date:** 2026-09-01  
**Scope:** One endnote whose verification exposed dependent errors in Chapter 19, Appendix Part 1, and two working documents.

## Result

| Endnote | Risk | Result | Finding and action |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| `schleicher-1868-fable` | P0 | Corrected | Added exact pages for Robins and Morpurgo Davies. Corrected the claims that Schleicher invented the reconstruction asterisk and placed one before every form in the original 1868 fable. The manuscript now distinguishes earlier uses of the sign, Schleicher's systematic reconstruction apparatus, and his extension of reconstruction into connected prose. |

## Tests Performed

1. Confirmed the 1868 publication title, journal, volume, and pages.
2. Inspected the original fable's typography and confirmed that its forms are not individually starred.
3. Located Robins's attribution to Schleicher at printed page 179.
4. Located Morpurgo Davies's discussion of the conventional attribution and earlier uses at pages 167 and 185.
5. Compared the revised full note with its Short form and all manuscript deployments.
6. Corrected active and queued working material that could have restored the superseded claim.
7. Completed full- and short-endnote assemblies.
