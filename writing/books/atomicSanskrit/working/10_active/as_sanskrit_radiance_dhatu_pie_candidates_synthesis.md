## Corrections Applied to the Gemini Findings

1. **Pāṇinian conditions remain visible.** *Coḥ kuḥ* demonstrates a palatal-to-velar coordinate movement within Sanskrit. It does not directly derive Latin *genus* from Sanskrit *jan*.
2. **⟪सद्⟫ remains provisional.** Sanskrit visarga occurs in a defined boundary environment, while Greek initial *s → h* occurs elsewhere in the word. The physical resemblance does not erase that difference.
3. **English *th* is a fricative.** The *tan / thin* row cannot describe **t → th** as simple aspiration.
4. **The *vid / wit* family needs phonetic precision.** Sanskrit **v** and the **w** used in Germanic transcription do not automatically represent a major place shift.
5. **Grassmann's law is not the frame for Pāṇini 8.4.53.** The Sanskrit rule should be stated through its own operation and examples.
6. **No case requires “zero theoretical leaps.”** Every proposed cross-language direction remains a hypothesis until repeated families and transmission evidence establish the pattern.
