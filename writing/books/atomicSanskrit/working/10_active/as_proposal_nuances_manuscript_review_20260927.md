# Proposal Nuances: Preface and Manuscript Review

**Date:** 27 September 2026  
**Status:** Approved and implemented on 27 September 2026, including the removal plan in Section G. The review below retains the original before-and-after record; Section H records the completed changes. No PDFs generated.

**Current source:** [Penguin proposal](../../outreach/atomic_sanskrit_penguin_proposal.md)  
**Earlier review:** [Spoken-frame alignment review, 26 September](as_publisher_frame_manuscript_alignment_review.md)

## Recommendation

The proposal has improved the explanation of the book more than it has changed its theses. The manuscript already develops language engineering, systems engineering, the two enemies, distributed correction, and the capacity to describe a changing world. What the proposal adds is a clearer account of why those things matter to someone who is interested in Sanātan but does not know Sanskrit.

The most useful additions are:

1. **Make the purpose unmistakable:** Sanātan's architecture is the subject; Sanskrit is the existing system through which readers examine it.
2. **Connect freedom to स्वराज्य (*svarājya*):** changing who governs is different from changing the relationship between government and society.
3. **State the human premise before the exception hammer:** most people can cooperate and exercise restraint; harmful conduct exists, but must it determine everyone's treatment?
4. **Explain why the mother's correction is more than a pronunciation preference:** the learner can understand, repeat, and eventually teach the check.
5. **Explain the connection to society through people:** standards, judgment, correction, responsibility, and teaching are relationships, not just properties of sounds.
6. **Introduce recursion through an operation the reader can follow, and connect recurring design principles to the fractal claim.** Do not simply replace every occurrence of “repeats” with “recursion.”
7. **Distinguish a living demonstration from the future polity:** the language system exists; later volumes develop the wider polity from first principles.
8. **Make ancient architecture serving new circumstances the point of generativity.** The numerical demonstration supports that purpose.

Keep the 21 chapters, seven parts, eclipse metaphor, technical examples, polemical voice, and existing figures. This is not a proposal to replace the book with the publisher's question-and-answer structure.

### P7. Show What the Mother's Correction Made Possible
