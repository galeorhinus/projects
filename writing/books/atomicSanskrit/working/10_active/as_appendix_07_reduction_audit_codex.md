**Manuscript:** `manuscript/as_3_07_architecture_in_vedas.md`
**Status:** Executed 2026-09-06. The appendix retains all four complete mantra analyses, the complete person-and-number concordance, the Chapter 11 and 12 source tables, and six secure examples from six later धातुः (*dhātuḥ*) groups.
**Current length:** 2,352 words, reduced from 3,185 words.

## Purpose

Appendix Part 7 supplies the Vedic evidence behind Chapters 11 and 12. It must:

1. identify the exact Rigvedic passages behind the principal verbs, derived words, endings, and sentences used in those chapters;
2. give a complete Vedic example for every combination of person and number;
3. preserve representative evidence for present, past, future, command, and possibility;
4. analyze complete Vedic passages so readers can see sounds, words, and sentences working together;
5. demonstrate that the Vedas already use the grammatical architecture that Pāṇini later documented;
6. separate that shared architecture from the additional resources examined in Chapter 16 and Appendix Part 8.

The appendix does not need to reteach examples already explained in Chapters 11 and 12. It also does not need to repeat the same conclusion after each table and again at the end.

## Ownership Check

- **Chapter 11** teaches five procedures by which a धातुः (*dhātuḥ*) becomes a verb. Appendix Part 7 should retain the source concordance and the wider person, number, time, and mode evidence.
- **Chapter 12** teaches the ⟪कृ⟫ (*kṛ*) family, sentence relations, RV 1.164.39c, and RV 2.23.1ab. Appendix Part 7 should retain the source concordance and the complete analysis of RV 1.164.39, but it should not repeat Chapter 12's complete parsing of RV 2.23.1ab.
- **Chapter 9 §9.10** explains the lineage-bounded **ळ** in **ईळे (*īḷe*)**. Appendix Part 7 needs the form and its local operation, but not a second paragraph restating the two-domain sonance argument.
- **Appendix Part 6** carries the numerical analysis of the ten गणाः (*gaṇāḥ*). Appendix Part 7 needs only enough classification to show that atoms from six later groups already form Vedic words.
- **Chapter 16 and Appendix Part 8** own the additional resources of the वैदिक (*vaidika*) domain. Appendix Part 7 should point to them without summarizing their argument.
