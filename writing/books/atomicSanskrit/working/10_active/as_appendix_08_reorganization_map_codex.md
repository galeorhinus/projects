## Dedicated Leṭ Section

Section 8.5 will replace the current compact *leṭ* discussion with a causal
sequence:

1. open with **ब्रवाणि** in Ṛgveda 6.16.16, where the Vedic *leṭ* form is
   identical to productive *laukika loṭ*;
2. use **तारिषत्** to establish what *leṭ* contributes when its form does not
   collide with *loṭ*;
3. distinguish those formal collisions from functional overlap with other
   productive *lakāras*;
4. explain how fixed words, position, syntax, meaning, inherited analysis, and
   sometimes accent keep the Vedic deployment bounded;
5. explain what the read-write *laukika* domain would gain and what additional
   burden it would inherit;
6. state the cost-to-benefit conclusion; and
7. place Pāṇini after the analysis as the documenter of the forms rather than
   the source of the collision test.

The Source and Reference Companion will retain the complete eighteen-coordinate
comparison. The body will explain the architecture without reproducing both
technical tables.
