## Language and Scope

The author rejected academic transitions such as “Arabic adds another comparison.” The revised chapter states what the authorities did, then explains how that affected the language. The new drafts follow the same rule: begin with the act or claim, not commentary announcing its role in an argument.

The pyramid remains an actor. The drafts retain the engineering claim, deliberate concealment, RAT, Pāṇini as decoder, the fourth Abrahamic religion, and the inside-out alternative. Technical words receive ordinary-language explanations. Epigraphs remain complete; their connection to the chapter is explained. Chapter 0's invocation is translated in its opening prose, as planned.

These drafts adapt the author's existing argument. This pass checks source correspondence and readability; it does not independently verify the historical, linguistic, or political claims anew.
