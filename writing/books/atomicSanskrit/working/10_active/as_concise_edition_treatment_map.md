## Cross-Chapter Checks

- Every core thesis has an adequate local explanation and demonstration.
- Each supporting argument has an explicit disposition; full development is not required for all fifty supporting theses.
- No later chapter depends on reasoning available only through a pointer.
- The metaphor is accompanied by literal explanation.
- Language engineering, systems engineering, and inside-out civilizational order remain connected.
- Simple vocabulary has not neutralized actors or diluted conclusions.

- Epigraphs retain their explanatory role, with accessible translations and clear connections to the prose.
