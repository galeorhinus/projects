# Vaidika-Laukika Variations: Qualified-Row Audit

**Status:** publication-treatment audit  
**Scope:** every row carrying **Q** in the 83-row evidence ledger  
**Purpose:** prevent rare, isolated, doubtful, and absence evidence from being
presented as equally productive Vedic options

## Audit Result

Twenty-six rows require some qualification. The qualification does not weaken
the complete inventory. It tells the reader what kind of evidence each row
contains.

The final figure source should use four labels:

- **RARE** — a real form in a small class or a few passages;
- **ISOLATED** — one or two reported occurrences;
- **DOUBTFUL** — the source doubts the form or its analysis;
- **ABSENCE** — the row records a missing or scarce later formation rather
  than another Vedic form.

## Rows That Must Be Split Before Illustration

Five rows cannot be drawn honestly as one undifferentiated line:

1. **SG-12:** documented **-avi** / doubtful **-ayi**;
2. **SG-25:** a rare class extension / one doubtful member;
3. **PL-05:** unstrengthened **-ias / -uas** / feminine **-īs**;
4. **PL-15:** rare alternate **-ām** / recitational resolution of **-ānām**;
5. **CL-10:** dominant participial dual **-ā** / isolated RV plural **-ānti**.

SG-04, SG-07, SG-09, and SG-16 can remain one numbered row only if the
qualification is attached to the specific subform rather than to the whole
category.

## Rows That Should Not Carry an Architectural Explanation

The following rows must remain visible without a DV code until stronger
evidence is found:

- **DOUBTFUL:** SG-12 **-ayi**, SG-25's doubtful member, DU-08;
- **ABSENCE:** SG-13;
- **ISOLATED and passage still open:** DU-06, DU-12, PL-18, NU-03;
- **RARE and passage still open:** SG-04, SG-05, SG-07, SG-09, SG-16,
  SG-17, SG-18, SG-25, DU-05, PL-05, PL-06, PL-15 simple **-ām**, and
  PL-17;
- **ISOLATED and exact passages still open:** PL-11 and CL-10 **-ānti**.

This treatment keeps the complete map while preventing the figure from
turning a documented variation into an invented purpose.
