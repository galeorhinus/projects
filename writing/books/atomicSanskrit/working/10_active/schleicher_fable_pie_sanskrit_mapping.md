# Schleicher's Fable — PIE-to-Sanskrit Mapping (Fast First Pass)

**Status:** Draft research note, not yet source-verified line-by-line. Confidence
flags mark how solid each correspondence is. Treat the ✓ rows as safe to build
on; treat ~ / ? / ✗ rows as needing a real verification pass (Dhātupāṭha
citation, attested Vedic form, or a named Indo-Europeanist source) before any
of this enters manuscript prose.

**Confidence key:**
- ✓ — direct or near-direct match to a real, attested Sanskrit word/dhātu
- ~ — real correspondence at the root level; the exact word-form Schleicher used differs from the attested Sanskrit form
- ? — uncertain; my own derivation, not checked against a source
- ✗ — weak or disputed cognate; flagging the mismatch rather than asserting a link

---

## 1. The text

August Schleicher's original 1868 PIE composition, *Avis akvāsas ka* ("The
Sheep and the Horses") — the first text ever composed in a reconstructed
proto-language:

> Avis, jasmin varnā na ā ast, dadarka akvams, tam, vāgham garum vaghantam,
> tam, bhāram magham, tam, manum āku bharantam. Avis akvabhjams ā vavakat:
> kard aghnutai mai vidanti manum akvams agantam. Akvāsas ā vavakant: krudhi
> avai, kard aghnutai vividvant-svas: manus patis varnām avisāms karnauti
> svabhjam gharmam vastram avibhjams ka varnā na asti. Tat kukruvants avis
> agram ā bhugat.

Translation: *A sheep that had no wool saw horses, one of them pulling a
heavy wagon, one carrying a big load, and one carrying a man quickly. The
sheep said to the horses: "My heart pains me, seeing a man driving horses."
The horses said: "Listen, sheep, our hearts pain us when we see this: a man,
the master, makes the wool of the sheep into a warm garment for himself. And
the sheep has no wool." Having heard this, the sheep fled into the plain.*

**Word count, confirmed by direct tokenization:** 57 total word-occurrences,
44 distinct orthographic forms (case-folded; "vividvant-svas" counted as one
hyphenated form, matching Schleicher's own orthography).

## 2. The 44-form mapping

| # | Schleicher's form | Gloss | Sanskrit correspondent | Conf. | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | avis | sheep | **अवि (avi)** | ✓ | Well-attested Rigvedic noun. The deeper root (possible link to ⟪अव्⟫ av, "protect") is disputed — leave that claim out. |
| 2 | jasmin | in which (loc.) | **यस्मिन् (yasmin)** | ✓ | Locative singular of यद् (yad); near-exact match. |
| 3 | varnā | wool | **ऊर्णा (ūrṇā)** | ~ | Same PIE source (*h₂wĺ̥h₁neh₂), but Sanskrit's actual reflex is ūrṇā, not a v-initial form. Don't claim varnā = a real Sanskrit word. |
| 4 | na | not | **न (na)** | ✓ | Exact. |
| 5 | ā | (prefix/particle) | **आ (ā)** | ✓ | Exact — common Sanskrit prefix. |
| 6 | ast | was | **आसीत् (āsīt)** | ✓ | Imperfect 3sg of ⟪अस्⟫ (as), "to be." |
| 7 | dadarka | saw | **ददर्श (dadarśa)** | ✓ | Perfect 3sg of ⟪दृश्⟫ (dṛś). Near-verbatim reproduction of the real Sanskrit form. |
| 8 | akvams | horses (acc. pl.) | **अश्वान् (aśvān)** | ✓ | From अश्व (aśva). |
| 9 | tam | that/him | **तम् (tam)** | ✓ | Exact. |
| 10 | vāgham | wagon | **वह (vaha)** | ~ | Root-level match via ⟪वह्⟫ (vah), "to carry." No single attested Sanskrit noun "vāgha." |
| 11 | garum | heavy | **गुरुम् (gurum)** | ✓ | From गुरु (guru), "heavy" (also "teacher"). |
| 12 | vaghantam | pulling | **वहन्तम् (vahantam)** | ✓ | Present participle acc. of ⟪वह्⟫ (vah). Near-exact. |
| 13 | bhāram | load | **भारम् (bhāram)** | ✓ | From भार (bhāra), built on ⟪भृ⟫ (bhṛ). |
| 14 | magham | big | **महत्/महान्तम् (mahat/mahāntam)** | ~ | Root-level match to मह् (mah), "be great." Schleicher's exact form isn't the attested accusative. |
| 15 | manum | man (acc.) | **मनुम् (manum)** | ✓ | From मनु (Manu). Exact. |
| 16 | āku | quickly | **आशु (āśu)** | ~ | Real cognate, but Schleicher's k stands where Sanskrit has palatal ś. |
| 17 | bharantam | carrying | **भरन्तम् (bharantam)** | ✓ | Present participle acc. of ⟪भृ⟫ (bhṛ). Exact. |
| 18 | akvabhjams | to/with the horses | **अश्वेभ्यः / अश्वैः** | ~ | Root अश्व confirmed; the *-bʰi̯os ending is a PIE reconstruction, not the attested Sanskrit case-ending. |
| 19 | vavakat | said | **⟪वच्⟫ (vac)** | ~ | Right root, but Sanskrit's actual perfect is the irregular उवाच (uvāca), not a reduplicated vavakat-type form. |
| 20 | kard | heart | **हृद्/हृदय (hṛd/hṛdaya)** | ✓ | Regular sound correspondence (PIE *ḱ → Skt h in this environment). |
| 21 | aghnutai | pains | **अघ (agha)** | ~ | Noun "evil, distress" is well-attested; a matching verbal root is not commonly cited — root-level link only. |
| 22 | mai | to me | **मे (me)** | ✓ | Enclitic dative/genitive. |
| 23 | vidanti | they see | **विदन्ति (vidanti)** | ✓ | 3pl present of ⟪विद्⟫ (vid). Exact. |
| 24 | agantam | driving | **अजन्तम् (ajantam)** | ~ | Root ⟪अज्⟫ (aj) confirmed; Schleicher's g vs. Sanskrit's j reflects a centum-leaning choice, not the attested Sanskrit form. |
| 25 | akvāsas | horses (nom. pl.) | **अश्वासः (aśvāsaḥ)** | ✓ | Vedic-domain (*chandasi*) nominative plural of अश्व, distinct from the *laukika* -āḥ ending — a domain variant, not an earlier stage of it. |
| 26 | vavakant | said (3pl) | **⟪वच्⟫ (vac)** | ~ | Same caveat as #19; attested Sanskrit 3pl perfect is ऊचुः (ūcuḥ). |
| 27 | krudhi | listen! | *(uncertain)* | ? | Needs checking — possible link to the hear-root (Skt श्रु, śru; imperative शृणु, śṛṇu) but the match is not clean. Don't assert this one without a real source. |
| 28 | avai | O sheep (voc.) | **अवे (ave)** | ✓ | Vocative of अवि. |
| 29 | vividvant-svas | (we) knowing | **विद्वांसः (vidvāṃsaḥ)** | ~ | Perfect active participle of ⟪विद्⟫ (vid); the -svas piece is a separate reflexive particle (cf. स्व, sva). |
| 30 | manus | man (nom.) | **मनुः (manuḥ)** | ✓ | Exact. |
| 31 | patis | master | **पतिः (patiḥ)** | ✓ | Exact — one of the best-known Sanskrit nouns. |
| 32 | varnām | wool (acc.) | **ऊर्णाम् (ūrṇām)** | ~ | Same caveat as #3. |
| 33 | avisāms | of the sheep (gen. pl.) | **अवीनाम् (avīnām)** | ~ | Root confirmed; ending is an older PIE genitive-plural reconstruction. |
| 34 | karnauti | makes | **करोति / कृणोति (karoti / kṛṇoti)** | ✓ | 3sg present of ⟪कृ⟫ (kṛ) — the book's flagship atom. Schleicher's -nauti actually matches the real *chandasi*-domain present kṛṇoti almost exactly — the same atom's Vedic-domain form, not an earlier stage of *karoti*. Worth a callout. |
| 35 | svabhjam | for himself | **स्व (sva)** | ~ | Root confirmed (reflexive "own/self"); exact case-form is a PIE reconstruction. |
| 36 | gharmam | warm | **घर्मम् (gharmam)** | ✓ | From घर्म (gharma), "heat" — a real, well-known Vedic ritual term. |
| 37 | vastram | garment | **वस्त्रम् (vastram)** | ✓ | From ⟪वस्⟫ (vas), "to wear." Exact. |
| 38 | avibhjams | to the sheep | **अविभ्यः (avibhyaḥ)** | ~ | Same ending caveat as #18. |
| 39 | ka | and | **च (ca)** | ✓ | Regular sound correspondence (PIE *kʷe → Skt ca). |
| 40 | asti | is | **अस्ति (asti)** | ✓ | Exact — the textbook is/asti/esti/est cognate set. |
| 41 | tat | that (n.) | **तत् (tat)** | ✓ | Exact. |
| 42 | kukruvants | having heard/cried | *(uncertain)* | ? | Same caveat as #27 — flag, don't assert. |
| 43 | agram | plain/field | **अग्रम् (agram)** | ✗ | The Sanskrit word is real and the phonological match is exact, but अग्र actually means "point, tip, foremost part" — not "open plain." This looks like a semantic mismatch in Schleicher's own gloss, not a real correspondence for "field." |
| 44 | bhugat | fled | **⟪भुज्⟫ (bhuj)?** | ✗ | Weak. Sanskrit bhuj has "bend/curve" and "enjoy/eat" senses; the "flee" sense (PIE *bʰewg-) is much better preserved in Greek pheugō / Latin fugiō than in Classical Sanskrit. Don't claim a confident Sanskrit match here. |

**Headline count:** of the 44 forms, roughly **20 land as direct/near-exact
matches (✓)**, **17 are real correspondences at the root level with a
different attested word-form (~)**, and **7 are genuinely uncertain or weak
(?/✗)** and should not be used in the book without further checking.

**The one line worth building the argument around:** #34, *karnauti* ↔
*karoti*/*kṛṇoti* — Schleicher's reconstructed PIE verb for "makes" is
functionally identical to a real *chandasi*-domain present-tense form of
⟪कृ⟫ (kṛ), the atom this whole book is built around — the same engineered
atom, deployed in its Vedic-domain form, not a superseded stage of it.
That's not resemblance through common descent — that's Schleicher visibly
working from Sanskrit.

## 4. Pokorny's dictionary — the "2,222" figure is not Pokorny's

This needs a correction before it goes near the book. Three different
numbers are in circulation, and they measure three different things:

| Figure | What it actually is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **~2,044** | Root entries in Pokorny's *Indogermanisches etymologisches Wörterbuch* (1959), per Douglas Adams' assessment. Adams notes some entries are "pretty dubious," and that Anatolian, Tocharian, and Albanian are underrepresented since Pokorny worked mainly from pre-war sources. | Cited in a Language Log discussion, ["Ur-etyma: how many are there?"](https://languagelog.ldc.upenn.edu/nll/?p=13311) |
| **~3,549** | A rough page-based estimate (≈3 entries/page × 1,183 pages) by Michael Witzel — larger because it counts derived nouns and other non-root headwords alongside true roots, not roots alone. | Same Language Log discussion |
| **2,222** | **Not Pokorny's number at all.** This is the count of "main entries" the University of Texas at Austin Linguistics Research Center's *Indo-European Lexicon* (IELEX) database curators selected from Pokorny's IEW when building their online database, decades after Pokorny's death. Their own page states it directly: *"we have selected 2,222 main entries from Pokorny's IEW."* | <https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/lex> |

**The correct claim for the book:** Pokorny's 1959 dictionary expanded
Schleicher's forty-some fable-roots into roughly **2,000 reconstructed
roots** (Adams' ~2,044, or Witzel's larger ~3,549 if counting all
headwords). The figure **2,222 belongs to a 21st-century UT Austin
digitization project's curatorial choice, not to Pokorny's own scope** —
attributing it to Pokorny directly would be a citation error.
