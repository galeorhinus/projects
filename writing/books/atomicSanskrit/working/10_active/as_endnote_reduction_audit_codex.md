## Rules

1. Length alone does not justify a cut. Tables, primary passages, bibliographic locators, and verification records remain when the note is their proper home.
2. Remove repeated body interpretation, repeated conclusions inside the same note, obsolete editorial discussion, and defensive qualifications that answer no live issue.
3. Preserve the source, locator, evidence, translation or calculation needed to understand it, and any limitation that changes the strength of the claim.
4. Do not alter manuscript body prose during this pass.
5. Preserve substantive removed material in `as_endnote_reduction_lost_and_found_codex.md`.
