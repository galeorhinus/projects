# Vaidika-Laukika Variations: High-Value Passage Concordance

**Status:** working source concordance  
**Purpose:** verify exact passages before any form or Designed Variation code
enters the appendix figures  
**Scope:** the strongest additions found after the first ten-example pilot

## Reading This File

Each entry keeps four kinds of evidence separate:

1. the grammatical category documented by Whitney;
2. the metrically restored *saṃhitāpāṭha*;
3. the relevant words in the *padapāṭha*; and
4. the local contribution demonstrated by the complete passage.

The English lines below are narrow working translations of the relevant
construction. They are not proposed as final translations of the whole mantra.

## 1. SG-06 — Shorter Dative ***शिश्वे (*śiśve*)***

**Category:** *ikārānta/ukārānta*, ***चतुर्थी एकवचनम्***  
**Comparison:** ***शिशवे / शिश्वे (*śiśave / śiśve*)***  
**Passage:** [RV 2.34.8c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV02#2.034.08c)

> *dhenur na śiśve svasareṣu pinvate*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *dhenuḥ | na | śiśve | svasareṣu | pinvate*

Working sense: “Like a cow, she swells for the young one in her own
dwelling.”

The transmitted pāda has twelve syllables. Substitution of laukika
***शिशवे (*śiśave*)*** would add a thirteenth. **MAT** and **ARR** are
demonstrated.

## 2. SG-11 / AC-04 — Locative ***वेदी (*vedī*)*** and *Pragṛhya*

**Category:** *ikārānta*, ***सप्तमी एकवचनम्***  
**Passage:** [RV 2.3.4b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV02#2.003.04b)

> *stīrṇaṃ rāye subharaṃ vedi asyām*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *stīrṇam | rāye | subharam | vedī iti | asyām*

Working sense: “Spread on this altar, easy to bear, for abundance.”

The *padapāṭha* marks ***वेदी (*vedī*)*** with *iti*. Its final **ī**
remains separate before the following **a** of ***अस्याम् (*asyām*)***.
The passage therefore demonstrates a preserved grammatical boundary in
recitation. **REL** and **REC** are supported.

## 3. SG-12 — *Ukārānta* Locative ***सानवि (*sānavi*)***

**Category:** *ukārānta*, ***सप्तमी एकवचनम्***  
**Comparison:** ***सानौ / सानवि (*sānau / sānavi*)***  
**Passage:** [RV 4.45.1b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV04#4.045.01b)

> *rathaḥ parijmā divo asya sānavi*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *rathaḥ | parijmā | divaḥ | asya | sānavi*

Working sense: “The all-traversing chariot is yoked on the summit of
heaven.”

The transmitted pāda has twelve syllables. Laukika ***सानौ (*sānau*)***
would remove one. **MAT** and **ARR** are supported for the documented
**-avi** form. Whitney's proposed *ikārānta* **-ayi** parallel remains
**DOUBTFUL** and receives no DV code.

## 4. SG-19 — Endingless Locative ***मूर्धन् (*mūrdhan*)***

**Category:** *an/man/van*-ending words, ***सप्तमी एकवचनम्***  
**Comparison:** ***मूर्धनि / मूर्धन् (*mūrdhani / mūrdhan*)***  
**Passage:** [RV 9.17.6b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV09#9.017.06b)

> *mūrdhan yajñasya kāravaḥ*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *mūrdhan | yajñasya | kāravaḥ*

Working sense: “The singers [place him] at the summit of the *yajña*.”

The transmitted Gāyatrī pāda has eight syllables. ***मूर्धनि
(*mūrdhani*)*** would add a ninth. **MAT** and **ARR** are demonstrated
for the endingless locative.

## 5. SG-27 / PL-16 / AC-04 — Pronoun ***युष्मे (*yuṣme*)***

**Category:** first- and second-person pronouns, dative-locative e-form  
**Passage:** [RV 8.18.19c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV08#8.018.19c)

> *yuṣme id vo api ṣmasi sajātiye*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *yuṣme iti | it | vaḥ | api | smasi | sajātye*

Working sense: “We belong among you, in kinship.”

The *padapāṭha* marks ***युष्मे (*yuṣme*)*** as *pragṛhya*. The final
**e** does not combine with the following **i** of ***इत् (*it*)***.
**REL** and **REC** are demonstrated. This passage verifies the e-form; it
does not yet verify the separate **-bhya** subcategory in PL-16.

## 6. DU-09 — Five Distinct Second-Person Dual Relations

Whitney §492b identifies five distinct Vedic dual relations where laukika
Sanskrit combines them into three forms. The following passages now verify all
five:

| Relation | Vedic form | Exact passage | Local construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| ***प्रथमा*** | ***युवम् (*yuvam*)*** | [RV 7.74.2a](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV07#7.074.02a) | *yuvaṃ citraṃ dadathur bhojanaṃ narā* — “You two gave splendid sustenance, O men.” |
| ***द्वितीया*** | ***युवाम् (*yuvām*)*** | [RV 6.60.7a–b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV06#6.060.07a) | *indrāgnī yuvām ime / abhi stomā anūṣata* — “Indra and Agni, these praises have sounded toward you two.” |
| ***तृतीया–चतुर्थी*** | ***युवभ्याम् (*yuvabhyām*)*** | [RV 7.68.4b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV07#7.068.04b) | *ūrdhvo vivakti somasud yuvabhyām* — “The Soma-presser proclaims upward for you two.” |
| ***पञ्चमी*** | ***युवत् (*yuvat*)*** | [RV 1.109.1c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV01#1.109.01c) | *nānyā yuvat pramatir asti mahyam* — “For me there is no other guiding thought apart from you two.” |
| ***षष्ठी–सप्तमी*** | ***युवोस् (*yuvos*)*** | [RV 4.44.2c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV04#4.044.02c) | *yuvor vapur abhi pṛkṣaḥ sacante* — “Nourishments attend the form of you two.” |

The separate forms make the relation audible rather than requiring one dual
form to cover several case functions. **SEM** and **REL** are demonstrated.

## 7. DU-10 — Two Instrumental Quantities

Whitney records both ***युवभ्याम् (*yuvabhyām*)*** and ***युवाभ्याम्
(*yuvābhyām*)***:

- [RV 7.68.4b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV07#7.068.04b):
  *yuvabhyām*.
- [RV 8.5.18c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV08#8.005.18c):
  *yuvābhyām bhūtu aśvinā*.
- [RV 5.64.4a](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV05#5.064.04a):
  *yuvābhyām mitrāvaruṇā*.

Both quantities are verified. A designed contribution has not yet been
demonstrated, so the figure should print no DV code for this row.

## 8. DU-11 — Compact Dual ***एनोः (*enoḥ*)***

**Category:** demonstrative dual, ***षष्ठी–सप्तमी***  
**Comparison:** ***एनयोः / एनोः (*enayoḥ / enoḥ*)***  
**Passage:** [RV 7.103.4a](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV07#7.103.04a)

> *anyo anyam anu gṛbhṇāti enor*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *anyaḥ | anyam | anu | gṛbhṇāti | enoḥ*

Working sense: “One of these two follows the other.”

The compact form preserves the dual genitive-locative relation. **SEM** and
**REL** are supported.

## 9. PL-02 — Feminine Plural ***वशासः (*vaśāsaḥ*)***

**Category:** *ākārānta* feminine, ***प्रथमा बहुवचनम्***  
**Comparison:** ***वशाः / वशासः (*vaśāḥ / vaśāsaḥ*)***  
**Passage:** [RV 6.63.9d](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV06#6.063.09d)

> *daśa vaśāso abhiṣāca ṛṣvān*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *daśa | vaśāsaḥ | abhiṣācaḥ | ṛṣvān*

Working sense: “Ten cows, accompanying the lofty ones.”

The transmitted pāda has eleven syllables. ***वशाः (*vaśāḥ*)*** would
remove one. **MAT** and **ARR** are demonstrated. The form remains **RARE**.

## 10. PL-13 / PL-21 — Pronominal ***येभिः (*yebhiḥ*)***

**Category:** relative pronoun, ***तृतीया बहुवचनम्***  
**Comparison:** ***यैः / येभिः (*yaiḥ / yebhiḥ*)***  
**Passage:** [RV 7.1.7b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV07#7.001.07b)

> *yebhis tapobhir adaho jarūtham*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *yebhiḥ | tapobhiḥ | adahaḥ | jarūtham*

Working sense: “With those heats with which you burned the aging one.”

The transmitted pāda has eleven syllables. ***यैः (*yaiḥ*)*** would
remove one. **MAT**, **ARR**, and **REL** are demonstrated.

## 11. NU-05 — Uninflected ***पञ्च (*pañca*)***

**Category:** numerals five and above with an oblique counted noun  
**Passage:** [RV 2.2.10c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV02#2.002.10c)

> *asmākaṃ dyumnam adhi pañca kṛṣṭiṣu*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *asmākam | dyumnam | adhi | pañca | kṛṣṭiṣu*

Working sense: “May our radiance shine over the five peoples.”

***पञ्च (*pañca*)*** keeps its nominative-accusative shape while
***कृष्टिषु (*kṛṣṭiṣu*)*** carries the locative relation. **SEM** and
**REL** are demonstrated.

## 12. NU-06 — Uninflected Collectives

### ***सहस्रम् ऋषिभिः (*sahasram ṛṣibhiḥ*)***

[RV 8.3.4a](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV08#8.003.04a):

> *ayaṃ sahasram ṛṣibhiḥ sahaskṛtaḥ*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *ayam | sahasram | ṛṣibhiḥ | sahaskṛtaḥ*

Working sense: “This one was made a thousandfold mighty by the *ṛṣis*.”

### ***शतम् पूर्भिः (*śatam pūrbhiḥ*)***

[RV 6.48.8c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV06#6.048.08c):

> *śatam pūrbhir yaviṣṭha pāhi aṃhasaḥ*

Working sense: “O youngest one, protect [us] from distress with a hundred
strongholds.”

In both constructions the collective numeral remains uninflected while the
associated noun carries the instrumental relation. **SEM** and **REL** are
demonstrated.

## 13. SG-01 — Repeated Instrumental ***यज्ञा (*yajñā*)***

**Category:** *akārānta*, ***तृतीया एकवचनम्***
**Comparison:** ***यज्ञेन / यज्ञा (*yajñena / yajñā*)***
**Passage:** [RV 6.48.1a-b](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV06#6.048.01)

> *yajñā-yajñā vo agnaye*
> *girā-girā ca dakṣase*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *yajñā-yajñā | vaḥ | agnaye | girā-girā | ca |
dakṣase*

Working sense: “Sacrifice by sacrifice for your Agni; song by song for the
capable one.”

Each pāda has eight syllables. ***यज्ञा (*yajñā*)*** has two syllables,
whereas ***यज्ञेन (*yajñena*)*** has three. Replacing both occurrences
would add two syllables and break the line. The repeated short instrumental
also answers the sound pattern of ***गिरा गिरा (*girā girā*)***. **MAT,
ARR,** and **RES** are demonstrated.

The metrically lengthened pronunciation **-enā** belongs to the **-ena**
family. It should not be counted as an independently selected third
declensional ending unless an exact grammatical use establishes that
distinction.

## 14. SG-02 — Instrumental Sequence ***हृदा मनसा मनीषा***

**Category:** *ākārānta* feminine, ***तृतीया एकवचनम्***
**Comparison:** ***मनीषया / मनीषा (*manīṣayā / manīṣā*)***
**Passage:** [RV 1.61.2c](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV01#1.061.02)

> *indrāya hṛdā manasā manīṣā*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *indrāya | hṛdā | manasā | manīṣā*

Working sense: “For Indra, with heart, with mind, and with inspired
thought.”

The passage places three instrumental forms together:
***हृदा (*hṛdā*), मनसा (*manasā*),*** and ***मनीषा (*manīṣā*)***.
The contracted ***मनीषा*** has three syllables; ***मनीषया
(*manīṣayā*)*** has four. The shorter form preserves the line while the
three **-ā** endings give the sequence a repeated audible close. **MAT** and
**RES** are demonstrated.

## 15. PL-12 — Adjacent ***रुद्रैः / रुद्रेभिः*** Selection

**Category:** *akārānta*, ***तृतीया बहुवचनम्***
**Comparison:** ***रुद्रैः / रुद्रेभिः (*rudraiḥ / rudrebhiḥ*)***
**Passages:** [RV 3.32.2d-3d](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV03#3.032.02)

> *sajoṣā rudrais tṛpad ā vṛṣasva*
> *pibā rudrebhiḥ sagaṇaḥ suśipra*

Relevant *padapāṭha*: *sa-joṣāḥ | rudraiḥ | tṛpat | ā | vṛṣasva*;
*piba | rudrebhiḥ | sa-gaṇaḥ | su-śipra*

Working sense: “In harmony with the Rudras, drink your fill and grow
strong. Drink with the Rudras and your company, O fair-cheeked one.”

The two adjacent verses use both instrumental-plural endings. Each quoted
pāda has eleven syllables. Substituting ***रुद्रेभिः*** for
***रुद्रैः*** in the first would produce twelve; substituting
***रुद्रैः*** for ***रुद्रेभिः*** in the second would produce ten.
The paired passages therefore show selection within the Vedic corpus itself:
each ending supplies the quantity required by its own line. **MAT** and
**ARR** are demonstrated.

This pair also establishes an important interpretive boundary. The
comparison with the laukika productive form describes deployment in new
composition. It does not imply that ***रुद्रैः*** arose after
***रुद्रेभिः***; the Veda preserves both.

## 16. RV 10.63 — Three Variations in One Hymn Cluster

[RV 10.63.2-4](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/books/rigveda/RV10#10.063.02)
brings three high-value forms into a compact span:

| ID | Form | Local evidence | Demonstrated contribution |
|---|---|---|---|
| PL-07 | ***विश्वा (*viśvā*)*** at 10.63.2a | Two syllables; ***विश्वानि (*viśvāni*)*** would add one. The verse itself contains several fuller **-āni** forms. | **MAT · ARR** |
| DU-01 | ***देवा (*devā*)*** at 10.63.2b | ***देवा / देवौ*** have the same syllable count and weight. | **OPEN** |
| PL-01 | ***देवासः (*devāsaḥ*)*** at 10.63.4b | Three syllables; ***देवाः (*devāḥ*)*** would remove one from the twelve-syllable line. | **MAT · ARR** |

The cluster is valuable because it shows that different forms coexist in one
small Vedic field. It also prevents a metrical explanation from being forced
onto DU-01, where the comparison changes vowel quality without changing
quantity.

## Passage-Verification Boundary

An exact occurrence moves a row from **G** to **G/P**. It does not by itself
justify a Designed Variation code. The figure may print a DV code only when
the complete passage demonstrates the contribution, as the syllable count
does for ***शिश्वे, सानवि, मूर्धन्, वशासः,*** and ***येभिः***, or when the
operation itself makes the relation or recitational boundary visible, as with
the five dual pronoun forms and the *pragṛhya* examples.
