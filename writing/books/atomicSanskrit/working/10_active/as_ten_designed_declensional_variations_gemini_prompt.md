# Gemini Research Prompt: Independent Verification of Ten Vedic Declensional Variations

I need an independent, web-based verification of ten proposed differences
between the *vaidika* and *laukika* domains of Sanskrit. The result will support
Appendix Part 8, **One Architecture, Two Domains**, in *Atomic Sanskrit*.

This is an adversarial source audit. Do not preserve my premise when the sources
contradict it. Verify each form from the Vedic corpus, determine what it
contributes in its actual passage, and identify which proposed examples are
strong enough for publication.

Save or return the completed research under this filename:

`as_ten_designed_declensional_variations_gemini_research.md`

## Search strategy

Search exact Devanagari and IAST forms in reliable Vedic corpora. Useful
starting points may include:

- Vedic Heritage Portal;
- GRETIL;
- TITUS;
- SanskritDocuments Vedic texts;
- searchable Saṃhitā and *padapāṭha* editions;
- Internet Archive scans of Vedic texts, Prātiśākhyas, Śikṣā works, Kāśikā,
  Mahābhāṣya, and traditional commentaries;
- Aṣṭādhyāyī resources that preserve the Sanskrit sūtra and commentary.

Use Macdonell and Whitney to locate categories, but trace every publishable
claim back to an exact passage and, where possible, a Hindu analytical source.
Provide direct links to the page or scan, not a search-result page.

Search each variant separately. Do not assume that a grammar's paradigm proves
that the form appears in a surviving passage.

## Non-negotiable accuracy rules

- Never invent a mantra, prose passage, accent, *padapāṭha*, translation,
  sūtra, commentary, page number, or link.
- If an exact passage cannot be located, write **NOT FOUND** and list every
  source and query checked.
- Quote only enough source text to identify the form and context.
- Distinguish Saṃhitā text, *padapāṭha*, edited *padaccheda*, and modern
  normalization.
- Distinguish a widespread Vedic option from an isolated, doubtful,
  lexical, or śākhā-specific form.
- Do not use *archaic*, *older*, *later development*, or similar chronology as
  an explanation.
- Do not describe Pāṇini as creating, codifying, fixing, or standardizing a
  Vedic form. He documents forms already present.
- Use *ending class* or ***akārānta, ākārānta, ikārānta,*** and so on. Do not
  use the botanical term *stem*.
- Meter is not a default explanation. Scan the complete *pāda* before claiming
  that a variation satisfies meter.
- If two forms have the same syllable count or weight, say so explicitly.
- A correct **NOT FOUND** or **purpose remains open** is preferable to an
  elegant theory.

## Designed Variation codes

| Code | Category |
|---|---|
| **SON** | Sonomeric selection and distinguishability |
| **MAT** | Syllable count, *mātrā,* and *laghu-guru* |
| **SVR** | Pitch architecture |
| **REL** | Audible grammatical boundaries and recoverable relations |
| **ARR** | Poetic or compositional arrangement |
| **SEM** | Compact semantic distinctions |
| **RES** | Sound pattern and resonance |
| **REC** | Melodic or recitational function |
| **AUD** | Error detection and overlapping audit checks |
| **FUN** | A function specific to a Veda, Vedic prose setting, or mode of use |

For every code, mark:

- **Confirmed**
- **Probable**
- **Open**
- **Rejected**

The passage must support the assignment. The mere existence of a shorter form
does not prove **ARR**; the complete meter or composition must show that
function. Likewise, a different vowel does not prove **SVR** unless the accent
supports the claim.
