# Vaidika-Laukika 83-Row Audit: Review Memo

## What the Six Passes Completed

The audit examined every row in the complete declensional inventory:

1. 29 *ekavacanam* rows;
2. 12 *dvivacanam* rows;
3. 21 *bahuvacanam* rows;
4. 10 word-class rows;
5. 7 numeral rows;
6. 4 accent and recitation rows.

All 83 IDs are present once. Every row now has a grammatical source trail in
`as_vaidika_laukika_83_row_evidence_ledger.md`.

## What Changed

The most important correction concerns SG-01. The earlier audit could not find
an exact passage for Whitney's ***यज्ञा (*yajñā*)*** example and therefore
left the category open. A broader search located Whitney's other example,
***महित्वा (*mahitvā*)***, at RV 2.15.6a, 6.67.3d, and 8.25.18c. At RV
2.15.6a the short instrumental preserves an eleven-syllable pāda; ordinary
***महित्वेन (*mahitvena*)*** would add a twelfth syllable. SG-01 is therefore
figure-ready through ***महित्वा***.

The expanded audit found four kinds of qualified evidence that the final
figures must display differently:

- **RARE** forms in a small class or a few passages;
- **ISOLATED** forms reported once or twice;
- **DOUBTFUL** forms whose source questions the reading or analysis;
- **ABSENCE** rows that describe a distribution rather than another Vedic
  ending.

DU-08 is the clearest doubtful row. SG-13 is an absence/distribution row.
SG-12, SG-25, PL-05, PL-15, and CL-10 each combine subclaims with different
evidentiary strength and need split notes or subrows.

## What the Audit Does Not Claim

The completed passes verify the category map and extend the concordance. They
do not supply 83 exact passages. A grammar's statement that a form exists
cannot establish why a particular Vedic passage uses it.

The publication figures should therefore distinguish:

- **verified category**;
- **verified passage**;
- **verified local contribution**;
- **qualified or doubtful evidence**.

Tentative DV codes can remain in the research plan with question marks. The
publication figures should print only codes supported by a passage or by the
operation visible in the form itself.
