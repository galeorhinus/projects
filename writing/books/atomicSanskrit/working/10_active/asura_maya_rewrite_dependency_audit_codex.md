## Replacement Narrative

Sanskrit has two distinct words on the sound-form **asura**: ***asu-ra*** and the privative ***a-sura***. The Rigveda transmits that surface form across praised, neutral, and hostile contexts without assigning each occurrence between the two words. Yaska applies the life-breath analysis while commenting on hostile *asuras* in RV 10.53.4, while the Kauthuma privative division occurs in the praised context corresponding to RV 7.6.1.

The book can therefore state that Sanskrit has ***asu-ra*** and ***a-sura*** without claiming that the Rigveda demonstrably uses both or that each occurrence can be assigned to one of them. In the Svarbhanu episode, RV 8.96.9, RV 10.124.5, and RV 10.138.3, the lexical assignment remains less important than the action.

The argument begins with **maya**. Power does not decide alignment. Its purpose and use do. The pyramid hides this Vedic demand for discernment by converting descriptions of capacity, conduct, or achievement into factional identities. With *asura*, **avarana** conceals the actor's actions and **vikshepa** projects a fixed identity. The Racial Arya Thesis performs the same substitution with *arya*. Recovering the action beneath the identity is an exercise of **viveka**. The lexical uncertainty around ***asu-ra*** and ***a-sura*** should appear only after this argument has been established.

## Executive Finding

The old thesis is not spread evenly across the manuscript. It has four levels of dependency:

1. **Core proof chain:** Chapter 3 section 3.6.
2. **Contact-history reuse:** Chapter 19 section 19.8 and Figure 19.9.
3. **Callbacks:** the Prologue, Part I opener, Chapters 4 and 18, and the thesis summary.
4. **Evidence apparatus:** approximately eighteen endnotes, several active plans, and two copies each of the Figure 19.9 master and promoted SVG.

Most uses of *asuric pyramid*, *asuric action*, Purana character names, and **asuri maya** do not depend on the old lexical thesis. They should not be changed merely because they contain the word *asura*.
