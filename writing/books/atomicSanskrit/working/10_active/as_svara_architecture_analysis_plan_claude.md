## 2. The gap the movement fills

Four logical pieces are missing:

1. **The two-layer structure.** The vowels are not a flat set of short/long pairs. They are *simple vowels* (**समानाक्षर**, *samānākṣara*: अ इ उ ऋ ऌ) plus *junction-vowels* (**संध्यक्षर**, *saṃdhyakṣara*: ए ऐ ओ औ) that are **generated** by combination.
2. **The generation engine.** ए/ओ/ऐ/औ are produced by combining अ/आ with इ/उ (the *guṇa/vṛddhi* logic). The book shows the sibling operation इ+अ→**य** (a semivowel) but never the vowel operation अ+इ→**ए** (a compound vowel).
3. **The three logics of absence.** Consonants are excluded one way (left in the margin). Vowels are "missing" three different ways — and two of them are *stronger* than exclusion.
4. **The unification.** The simple vowels इ उ ऋ ऌ are the shared source of *both* the semivowels (य व र ल) *and* the compound vowels (ए ओ). The vowel/consonant boundary is itself an output of the combination rules.

## 3. The logical spine (the argument, in order)

This is the heart of the plan. The movement is one dichotomy→reframe, executed in three moves.

**The binary the reader arrives with.** Sanskrit's vowels are either (a) a tidy set of short/long pairs, or (b) a slightly irregular set with odd gaps — no short *e*, no short *o*, and an अ that "isn't quite" a short आ. Framing (b) reads the gaps as *defects*.

**The reframe.** Neither. The vowel inventory is the **exact closure of a two-layer generation system** — five simple vowels and a combination rule. The "pairs" are Layer 1. The "gaps" are precisely where Layer 2's compounds live, or where a single place carries two values by design. **The absences are as principled as the presences** — the signature of a *generated* inventory, not a collected one. (This mirrors §9.8's "engineering by exclusion" for consonants and raises it: consonant absences are curated; vowel absences are *generated*.)

**Move 1 — The vowels are a generated grid.**
- Layer 1: five simple vowels, each at one of the **five mouth-stations already mapped in §9.2** — अ (throat), इ (palate), उ (lip), ऋ (retroflex), ऌ (dental). The vowels and the consonant *vargas* share **one** place-axis. Each simple vowel takes the mātrā ladder (§9.6).
- Layer 2: the junction-vowels are built, not listed — अ+इ→ए, अ+उ→ओ, आ+इ→ऐ, आ+उ→औ. State these as combination facts (the sandhi logic), not as rules by number.
- **The clincher is a name, not a sūtra.** The disciplines call ए **कण्ठतालु** (throat + palate) and ओ **कण्ठोष्ठ** (throat + lip). The place-name *spells out the combination*: a throat-plus-palate vowel is two positions fused. That is the whole reason it exists and the whole reason it behaves as it does. (This is exactly the "focus on logic" register — the evidence is the articulatory name, available to any reader, no grammar apparatus required.)

**Move 2 — Three ways a vowel coordinate stays empty.** Each "missing" sound is missing for a stated structural reason, and the reasons are of three kinds:
1. **Place already assigned (economy).** The throat vowel is अ/आ; Sanskrit adds no second throat-vowel (the [ɯ] that §9.8 already notes is absent). One place, its coordinates already spent.
2. **Forbidden by generation (the strong case).** There is no short ए and no short ओ **because** ए and ओ are compounds (अ+इ, अ+उ). A compound cannot occupy a one-*mātrā* slot — it already contains two vowels. The absence is not a decision to exclude; it is a *logical consequence of how the sound is built*. Nothing could fill that cell without contradicting the rule that generates the vowel.
3. **Quality patched (the अ/आ case).** अ is contracted (*saṃvṛta*) in speech, but the system runs it as open (*vivṛta*) so that अ+अ→आ behaves, then restores it at the exit (the "अ अ" close, **already in §9.8**). So the a-place deliberately carries one contracted short value and one open long value — which is exactly why "long अ" and "short आ" have no independent coordinates. Not a gap; a design decision, documented by its own patch.
- **The proof the exclusion is a choice, not an inability:** short *e* and short *o* are perfectly pronounceable — the southern languages (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada) and the Prākṛta forms carry them freely. Sanskrit leaves those cells empty on purpose. (Geography + named languages per the classification rule; never a family-tree label. Parallel to §9.9's consonant version — "the mouth can make it; the architecture declines it.")

**Move 3 — Where the garland unfolds (the unification climax).**
- The simple vowels इ, उ, ऋ, ऌ are **amphibious**. Positioned as the tail of a junction, they fuse into compound vowels (अ+इ→ए) — the nucleus role. Positioned as the head before a vowel, the same simple vowels become the semivowels य, व, र, ल (इ+अ→य, **already in §9.8**) — the edge/contact role.
- **Same simple vowels, two outputs, decided by position.** The *antaḥstha* consonants and the *saṃdhyakṣara* vowels are two faces of one source. The line between "vowel" and "consonant" is not a wall the inventory starts with; it is drawn by the combination rules themselves.
- **Payoff:** the whole garland — nuclei, edges, compounds, and their measures — unfolds from a handful of simple vowels and a set of operations. This is the book's generative signature at the vowel scale, feeding the mouth → sonomer → *akṣara* → … scale-chain. Close on a verdict that rhymes with §9.8's "This is engineering by exclusion."

## 5. Section-by-section draft plan

Each section: **one claim → the logic → what it reuses → what it adds → close.** Register: causal-expository, named-agent (Sanskrit / the *varṇamālā* / the architecture act; not "the analysis shows"). Devanag* anchor on first use, IAST after.

### §9.9 The Svaras Are Built, Not Listed
- **Claim:** the vowel list is the output of five simple vowels and a rule, not fourteen independent entries.
- **Logic:** name the two classes (*samānākṣara* / *saṃdhyakṣara*); place the five simple vowels on the five stations of §9.2; state the four combinations (अ+इ→ए, अ+उ→ओ, आ+इ→ऐ, आ+उ→औ); land the place-name clincher (कण्ठतालु / कण्ठोष्ठ).
- **Reuse:** §9.6 mātrā table; §9.2 five stations; §9.7 the 14-vowel axis.
- **Add:** the two-layer split; the generation engine; the place-name evidence.
- **Close:** the garland's vowels are generated, so their shape is a result, not a choice of what to collect.

### §9.10 Three Ways a Vowel Coordinate Stays Empty
- **Claim:** every absent vowel is absent for a structural reason, of three distinct kinds.
- **Logic:** the three logics (place-spent / forbidden-compound / quality-patched), each stated as a consequence of a rule already on the table; then the pronounceability proof (southern + Prākṛta short *e/o*).
- **Reuse:** §9.8's अ/आ material and the absent [ɯ]; §9.9's Engineered-Margin logic.
- **Add:** the "forbidden by generation" case (the strong one) and the naming of the three kinds.
- **Close:** consonants exclude by leaving the margin unselected; vowels exclude by generation and by patch — the tighter engineering.

### §9.11 Where the Garland Unfolds
- **Claim:** the vowel grid and the consonant grid are one system, joined at the semivowels and the compound vowels.
- **Logic:** the amphibious इ/उ/ऋ/ऌ — tail→compound-vowel, head→semivowel; same source, two roles by position.
- **Reuse:** §9.8's इ+अ→य derivations (explicitly — "the operation §9.8 used to explain य is the sibling of the one that makes ए").
- **Add:** the vowel-side operation (अ+इ→ए) placed beside the consonant-side one (इ+अ→य), and the verdict that the vowel/consonant line is generated.
- **Close:** the whole garland unfolds from a few simple vowels and a set of operations — the generative signature at the vowel scale.

## 7. Pitch and nasality — the multi-channel carrier (keep separate from the quality-gap logic)

The originating question asked whether the "missing" vowels relate to the Vedic pitch. **They do not, and the body must not imply a causal link.** Pitch is orthogonal. Handle it as a distinct, short beat — not inside Move 2.

- **The honest claim:** the engineered vowel is a *multi-channel carrier*. On one vowel the architecture runs, simultaneously and independently: **quality** (which vowel — the lexical channel), **quantity** (*mātrā* — for meter/*chandas*), **pitch** (*udātta/anudātta/svarita* — the accent channel), and **nasality** (*anunāsika*). Vedic recitation reads all channels at once; meter and accent together are the redundancy that holds the calibrant against drift.
- **The guardrail:** state plainly that pitch does not cause the quality gaps — the gaps come from the combination rules (Move 2), while pitch is a separate layer *added on top of* any vowel. Do not braid the two.
- **One permitted resonance, flagged as analogy:** the *svarita* is itself a *compound* pitch (a glide combining two levels), the way ए/ओ are compound vowels — the same design mind building compounds on two axes. Deploy as one sentence, marked analogy, not cause.
- **Placement:** either a short close to §9.9 or a one-paragraph extension of §9.6 (which already names the pitch triad). **Do not develop pitch fully here** — point to Chapter 13 (preservation), Chapter 14 (calibration), and Appendix Part 8 (the *vaidika/laukika* pitch treatment). Ch9's job is to *name* the channels and hand them off.

## 10. Voice / style guardrails

- **No chronology for Indic forms.** Never "ए developed from अ+इ," "ऋ is a shortened ॠ," "the short vowel came first." Use *one-mātrā and two-mātrā members of ivarṇa*; use *combines to*, *fuses into*, *is the compound of* — present tense, structural, the standing-system tense.
- **"Missing" is imprecise.** Use *open coordinate / unselected coordinate / a sound the architecture declines / handled by another operation* — matching §9.9's *Engineered Margin* vocabulary and the Codex plan's terminology.
- **Named-agent, causal-expository.** *Sanskrit places the throat vowel at अ; the combination rule fuses अ and इ into ए.* Not *the analysis reveals* / *the vowel system can be seen as*.
- **Devanagari as anchor, not wallpaper** — first use of each vowel class and each place-name gets Devanagari + IAST; running prose uses IAST.
- **Focus-on-logic discipline:** if a sentence's warrant is a sūtra number, move the warrant to the endnote and let the *combination fact* or *place-name* carry the body sentence.
