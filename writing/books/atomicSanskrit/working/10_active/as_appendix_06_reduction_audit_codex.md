## Purpose

Appendix Part 6 must preserve the numerical evidence behind the engineering argument:

1. Establish the 2,168-entry structural baseline after instructional markers are removed.
2. Show that 98.2 percent of the listed atoms contain one अक्षर (*akṣara*) and that three-particle forms dominate the inventory.
3. Present the eight recurring patterns found in sound choice, position, clustering, atomic construction, activation, and reach.
4. Demonstrate the inverse relation between particle count and two independently measured kinds of reach.
5. Show that a small high-reach core accounts for most recorded verb use and recurs across Vedic and लौकिक (*laukika*) corpora.
6. Compare sonomeric construction with the recurring procedures that turn atoms into verbs.
7. Point readers to the *Source and Reference Companion* for complete tables, scripts, correction history, and replication commands.

Chapters 10 and 11 already demonstrate the architecture through atoms and completed Vedic verbs. The full companion preserves the exhaustive research record. This appendix should present enough method for the numbers to be intelligible and enough evidence for the reader to judge the patterns. It does not need to explain the same dataset or conclusion several times.
