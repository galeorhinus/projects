A full rewrite of the chapter opening. Names the orthodoxy's *seven-move standard story* explicitly:
1. Aryan-pastoralist origin

### 3. Ch 4 §4.1 — *vyākaraṇam* etymology + *vaiyākaraṇa* role-title + Patañjali's five *prayojanāni*

Three new pieces of load-bearing evidence inserted in the *Mahābhāṣya* opener section:

- **The etymology of *vyākaraṇam* (व्याकरणम्)**: *vi-* (apart) + *ā-* (toward / fully) + *kṛ* (to do) = *taking-apart*, *unfolding-apart*, *de-composition not composition*. The activity Pāṇini performed is the precise opposite of engineering or codification.
- **The role-title *vaiyākaraṇaḥ* (वैयाकरणः)**: the Sanskrit tradition's designation for Pāṇini, Kātyāyana, Patañjali, and all the pre-Pāṇinian grammarians. Plural *vaiyākaraṇāḥ* (वैयाकरणाः). The tradition does NOT use *sthapati* (स्थपति, architect) or *nirmātṛ* (निर्माता, constructor) or anything cognate with *engineer*. The role-title is uniformly *analyst*.
- **Patañjali's five *prayojanāni* (पञ्च प्रयोजनानि)** from the *Paspaśāhnika*: *rakṣā* (रक्षा), *ūha* (ऊह), *āgama* (आगम), *laghu* (लघु), *asaṃdeha* (असंदेह) — preservation, modification, scriptural injunction, brevity, removal of doubt. **All five presuppose an already-existing engineered language.** None says *engineer a new language* or *codify a drifting one*.
- **The no-preface observation**: the *Aṣṭādhyāyī* opens at sūtra 1.1.1 *vṛddhir ādaic* (वृद्धिर् आदैच्) and runs ~4,000 sūtras without any first-person statement of authorial intent. Documenter-consistent silence; engineer-inconsistent.
- New paragraph in §4.5 connecting the *siddha* axiom to the *engineered-first* clause — *the same claim told from two angles*.

### 4. Ch 5 §5.6 — *What the Orthodoxy Calls "Variation in the Vedas"*

A new section between §5.5 (*Engineered Against Entropy*) and §5.7 (was §5.6 — *Saṃskṛtam — The Calibrant Envelope*). Walks the orthodoxy's *eight specific evidentiary claims for internal Vedic drift* and gives the engineering response for each:

1. Rgveda vs. Atharvaveda differences → ritual function difference, not drift
2. Mandala-by-Mandala variation in the Rigveda → compositional choice (meter, deity), not chronology
3. Saṃhitā / Brāhmaṇa / Āraṇyaka / Upaniṣad stratification → functional, not evolutionary
4. Prātiśākhya sandhi variation → engineered alternates, prescribed and preserved
5. Accent system "erosion" → chandasi mode active, bhāṣāyām mode different; matrix succeeding
6. Word-form variants → grammatically permitted alternates (Pāṇini's *vā*, *vibhāṣā*)
7. Śākala vs. Bāṣkala recensional differences → parallel preservation lineages, each held against drift
8. Vedic-Avestan parallels → *pratibimba*, not common ancestor

Unifying observation as standalone blockquote:
> ***The orthodoxy reads all variation as drift. The engineering thesis reads all variation as engineered design choices within the same architecture.***

### 6. Ch 10 §10.7 + §10.8 — Productivity analysis + Varṇa-Vāda synthesis

It predicts that small atoms do the most work.*

Three pieces of evidence (polysemy, OCP suppression, distributional gaps) push toward post-hoc assignment over intrinsic varna-charge. Sharpened polemic: *varṇa-vāda only makes sense if Sanskrit is engineered* — the Sanskrit tradition's internal debates (*varṇa-vāda*, *sphoṭa-vāda*, Yaska's *Nirukta* method, *Mīmāṃsā*'s *śabda-pramāṇa*) all presuppose the engineering thesis.
