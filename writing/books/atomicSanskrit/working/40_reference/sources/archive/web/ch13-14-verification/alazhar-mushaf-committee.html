What do you know about the Muṣḥaf Revision Committee at Al-Azhar Al-Sharif?

  • 10 September 2021

Have you ever thought about the secret of the great accuracy attached to proofreading the muṣḥaf, the printed copy of the Quran that you hold in your hands?

What is the reason behind such great care about the accuracy of the letters and diacritics in the muṣḥaf, despite their great abundance?

What happens if a copy of the Qur’an is found to include an inaccuracy or a distortion here or there?

What is the mechanism of the work of the committee, which grants permissions for publishing and circulating muṣḥafs in Egypt and the world?

Who are the members of this committee, and how are they chosen?

You will find the answers to all these questions in the short documentary film about "The Muṣḥaf Revision Committee at Al-Azhar Al-Sharif."
