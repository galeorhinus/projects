This presentation of A comparative dictionary of Indo-Aryan languages allows readers to search for all of the information in the indexes to the ink-print edition. Consequently, the indexes and concordance have not been included as separate text. Addenda and corrigenda have been applied to the main text to form a single, integrated sequence of entries.
Data for this dictionary was most recently updated in September 2022.
