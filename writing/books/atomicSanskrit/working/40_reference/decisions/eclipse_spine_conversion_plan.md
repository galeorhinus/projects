# Eclipse Spine Conversion Plan

*Working doc / single source of truth for the spine conversion. Decided 2026-06-20 (author + Claude + Codex converged). The eclipse becomes the book's sole controlling frame; the courtroom is removed from the visible spine. **No chapters move.** This is a spine-narration + light-insertion pass, not a restructure.*

## 1. The decision

- **Eclipse is the sole spine.** Sūrya (Sanskrit) is eclipsed by Svarbhānu (the asuric pyramid) and Rāhu (PIE); the caretakers (Atris) recover the Sun *turīyeṇa brahmaṇā* — by the fourth formulation (RV 5.40.6). The book is that fourth formulation.
- **The courtroom leaves the visible spine.** It was scaffolding from before the eclipse frame matured; it now duplicates the eclipse in the *thief's* idiom and in a defensive posture. The **rigor it was protecting lives in the evidence, not the metaphor** — every dhātupāṭha analysis, sound-field survey, Mitanni anchor, and refutation stays exactly as is.
- **The chapter sequence does not change.** The existing order already reveals the Sun correctly: false perception → self-category → sound → atoms → preservation → PIE removal → recovery.
- **The Eclipse ledger (the 7-row table) becomes the controlling device.** Part openers and transitions say, in effect, *"this part removes this layer of the eclipse / restores this Sun."*

## 2. Why (rationale, for the record)

1. **Native vs. borrowed frame.** A book whose thesis is "the West stole Sanskrit's category and recoded it in foreign frames" must not narrate *itself* in a Western juridical frame. The eclipse is the book's own idiom, from its own keystone hymn.
2. **Posture.** The courtroom = "the prosecution will prove its case to the tribunal" (defensive, asks a foreign authority to rule). The eclipse = "the Sun was always there; you were trained not to see it; look" (a civilization sure of itself).
3. **Fit.** Parts III–V are the engineering reveal, not "testimony in a trial." "Restore the Sun's sound-body / atoms / anti-entropy" fits the fractal build natively.
4. **Telos.** The book's real end is *restoration* (Make the World Ārya), not a verdict against the dogma. The eclipse makes the destination the spine.
5. **Method-tie.** *turīyeṇa brahmaṇā* — recovery by the fourth formulation, not by force — gives the spine an elegant self-description the courtroom never had.
6. **The skeptic.** Won by rigorous chapters under a confident frame, not by a trial metaphor that puts them on the defensive. (See guardrail 5.)

## 6c. Wave 3 *ṛṣis* and Atris — reconciliation

**Author posture:** This book is a Wave 3 instrument. Its work is Atri-work. Preserve the subtlety: do not state "I am an Atri" loudly. Let the mantra name the role and let the work stand inside that role.

**Candidate line to preserve:** *The mantra says the Atris found the Sun when others could not. This book belongs to that work: not as the work completed, but as one attempt to make the Sun visible again.*
