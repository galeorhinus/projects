# Chapter 9 Sonance Deployment — Lost and Found

This file preserves the undeployed ***sonomeron*** terminology removed from the
glossary when **sonance** and **off-grid sonance** were introduced.

## Removed Glossary Entry

> ### **sonomeron**
>
> **Book-coined English, optional engineering rendering.** A stable sonomeric
> cell: one vowel-centered acoustic unit that can contain one or more sonomers
> without blurring their identities. Sanskrit's own word is stronger and
> primary: **अक्षरम् (*akṣaram*)**, the imperishable unit. *Sonomeron* is used
> only when the engineering analogy needs a compact English label for the
> *akṣara* as an assembly of sonomers.
>
> **Sanskrit pair**: *akṣara*.
>
> **Use in book**: glossary-only unless a later diagram needs the engineering
> rendering. The main prose should continue to use *akṣara*.

## Removed Cross-Reference Under *Akṣaram*

> **Engineering rendering**: *sonomeron* — a sonomeric cell. The book keeps
> *akṣara* primary because the Sanskrit term combines the technical and
> civilizational force.
