---

## 1. What "fractal" means in this book

*Fractal* is deployed in its working sense — *same organizing principle
across scales* — not in the strict measure-theoretic / infinite-self-
similarity sense. The book shows one engineering law, **लाघव (*lāghava*)
— compression with full recoverability** — operating on six scales:

| Scale | Object | Engineering signature |
|---|---|---|
| Sound | *varṇa* / sonomer | Continuous gesture compressed to a measured, classified particle |
| Sound-unit | *akṣara* | Sonomers compressed into an imperishable vowel-centered unit |
| Atom | *dhātuḥ* | Sonomers + scaffold compressed into a semantic atom |
| Molecule | *śabda* | Atoms + bonds compressed into a recoverable lexical molecule |
| Rule | *sūtra* | Operational specification compressed to the smallest recoverable form |
| Rule-system | *Aṣṭādhyāyī* | Maximum specification, minimum text |

The same *lāghava* law governs every layer. That is the fractal claim.

---

## 2. The fractal claim in one sentence

> *Sanskrit honors the same engineering at every scale: sonomer, akṣara,
> dhātuḥ, śabda, sūtra, Aṣṭādhyāyī. The architecture is fractal.*

## 4. The Fractal Corollary

Companion to the **Atomic Corollary** named in Ch 10 §10.15.

> ***The Fractal Corollary.*** *Sanskrit's engineering is not local. The
> same compression-with-recoverability law that builds the* sūtra *also
> builds the* dhātuḥ*, the* akṣara*, the* śabda*, the* sūtra*, and the*
> Aṣṭādhyāyī *itself. The architecture is fractal: one engineering
> principle, recurring across every scale at which Sanskrit operates.*

Lands in Ch 10 §10.15 (paired with the Atomic Corollary) and may be
re-quoted in the epilogue.

2. **§10.15 The Atomic Corollary** — Add the **Fractal Corollary** as a

   > ***The Fractal Corollary.*** *Sanskrit's engineering is not local.
   > The same compression-with-recoverability law that builds the*
   > sūtra *also builds the* dhātuḥ*, the* akṣara*, the* śabda*, the*
   > sūtra*, and the* Aṣṭādhyāyī *itself. The architecture is fractal:
   > one engineering principle, recurring across every scale at which
   > Sanskrit operates.*

   > *The Atomic Corollary names the unit. The Fractal Corollary names
   > the law of its recurrence. The two are inseparable: an atom that

3. **§10.16 Atoms in a Table (verdict close)** — Re-state the fractal
