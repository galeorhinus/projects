# Figure 8.7 — Mahāprāṇa as Vertical Expansion

**Where it lives:** Chapter 8 §8.11 ("Breath Above the Base"), the chapter's
closing engineering reveal. §§8.6–8.8 ran the southern, forest-belt,
Western IE, and Central Asian surveys against Sanskrit's 23-cell base
*with the ten mahāprāṇa stop cells held aside*. §8.11 brings the
mahāprāṇa back: it shows that mahāprāṇa is not a separate or imported
set of sounds but a **vertical expansion** of the same five mouth-places
through breath pressure.

**The argument the figure must land:**
Sanskrit takes the base place-grid (5 places × voiceless/voiced stops)
and doubles the stop rows by adding voiceless-aspirated and
voiced-aspirated rows. The five mouth-stations stay identical. The
horizontal axis (place) does not grow. Sanskrit gets twice as many
stop sonomers without crowding the place-axis — by making breath
pressure into a second engineered axis.

**Title:** *Mahāprāṇa as Vertical Expansion*
**Subtitle:** *Sanskrit doubles the stop rows by breath pressure; the
place-axis is unchanged.*

---

## Layout

Two side-by-side panels with a large vertical arrow between them.
Same five place columns in both panels — column heads must be
visually identical (this is how the "no new mouth places" point
lands).

```
┌──────────────────────────┐         ┌──────────────────────────┐
│   Left panel             │   +     │   Right panel            │
│   Base · 10 stop cells   │   prāṇa │   After mahāprāṇa ·      │
│                          │   ↓     │   20 stop cells          │
│   5 cols × 2 rows        │  arrow  │   5 cols × 4 rows        │
└──────────────────────────┘         └──────────────────────────┘
```

## Left panel — "Base · 10 stop cells"

**Column heads (5):** BIL · DEN · RET · PAL · VEL
(Sanskrit's five stop places; same place abbreviations as the other
Ch 8 / App 4 figures.)

**Rows (2):**

| Row label | BIL | DEN | RET | PAL | VEL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| voiceless | प | त | ट | च | क |
| voiced | ब | द | ड | ज | ग |

**Cell style:** Match the book's existing tile idiom — rounded
rectangle, fill **#e6e3db**, no outline, Devanāgarī letter centered
in **#2b2b2d**. Same size and proportions as the cells in the App 4
surveys / Figure 8.1.

**Panel caption beneath:** *5 places × 2 voicing states · 10 cells.*

## Middle — vertical arrow + label

A large vertical arrow (pointing right toward the right panel)
labeled in two lines:

- **Line 1 (bold):** *+ prāṇa*
- **Line 2 (italic, smaller):** *(breath pressure as a new axis)*

The arrow should feel structural, not decorative — a clear "the
architecture adds something here" gesture. Plain right-pointing arrow
in **#2b2b2d**; the label sits above or beside it.

## Right panel — "After mahāprāṇa · 20 stop cells"

**Column heads (5):** BIL · DEN · RET · PAL · VEL
**Visually identical to the left panel** — same abbreviations, same
position, same size. This identity is the figure's point.

**Rows (4) — in varṇamālā order:**

| Row label | BIL | DEN | RET | PAL | VEL | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| voiceless | प | त | ट | च | क | existing |
| voiceless aspirated | फ | थ | ठ | छ | ख | **new** |
| voiced | ब | द | ड | ज | ग | existing |
| voiced aspirated | भ | ध | ढ | झ | घ | **new** |

The mahāprāṇa rows (voiceless aspirated + voiced aspirated) sit
**interleaved** between voiceless and voiced — not appended at the
bottom. This matches Sanskrit's actual *varṇamālā* row order and
makes the expansion read as engineered insertion, not as foreign
material grafted on.

**Cell style for the new mahāprāṇa rows:** Same base tint **#e6e3db**
and same dark Devanāgarī **#2b2b2d** as the existing rows — but flag
each new cell with a **thin accent ring** in **#9a9384** (1px) around
the rounded tile. The accent says "added by Sanskrit's engineering,"
not "held aside" and not "foreign." Optionally a small **↑ + breath**
glyph next to the row label on the two new rows.

**Cell style for the existing rows:** Plain tile, no accent ring.

**Panel caption beneath:** *5 places × 4 breath-voicing combinations
· 20 cells.*

## Visual conventions

**Typography (matches the book's other figures):**
- Title: Gentium Book Plus or Charter, bold, ~10.9 pt at 4.5″ wide
- Subtitle: italic, ~8 pt
- Place pills / column heads: ~9 pt
- Row labels: ~9 pt
- Panel captions: italic, ~7.4 pt
- Bottom caption: italic, ~7.4 pt
- Devanāgarī: Adobe Devanāgarī or Noto Sans Devanāgarī, ~10 pt for
  in-cell letters

**Color palette (grayscale only):**
- Background: cream **#f4f4f3**
- Text + arrows: dark gray **#2b2b2d**
- Cell tiles: rounded rectangles, fill **#e6e3db**, no outline,
  Devanāgarī in **#2b2b2d**
- Mahāprāṇa accent ring (right-panel new rows only): thin
  **#9a9384** outline, ~1 px
- Pill backgrounds (column heads): light fill **#ffffff** with
  letter-spacing on BIL · DEN · RET · PAL · VEL
- Grid lines (if shown): subtle **#dcdad4**

**Sizing:**
- Canvas: **4.5 in wide × ~4.8 in tall**
- Two panels of roughly equal horizontal weight separated by the
  arrow column; left panel narrower vertically (2 rows) than right
  (4 rows) but they share the same row-height
- Margins: 0.18 in left/right, 0.18 in top, 0.16 in bottom

## Things to avoid

1. **Do not show the nasal, antaḥstha, or ūṣman rows.** They are
   correct base cells but they dilute the expansion focus. This
   figure is about the stop matrix expansion only.
2. **Do not append the mahāprāṇa rows at the bottom of the right
   panel.** They must sit interleaved between voiceless and voiced
   to match the varṇamālā row order. Appending them at the end
   would visually imply they were added on top of a complete base,
   which is exactly the wrong story.
3. **Do not style the mahāprāṇa cells as "held aside" or "faded".**
   Figure 8.1 does that move (held aside for the survey). Figure 8.7
   is the *restoration* — mahāprāṇa returns as the second engineering
   layer, fully present and architecturally integrated.
4. **Do not vary the column heads between panels.** The "no new mouth
   places" argument depends on visible identity.
5. **Do not add a visarga / boundary-breath forward-pointer.** Ch 9
   §9.5 handles boundary breath.
6. **Do not use color accents.** Grayscale only.
7. **Do not over-decorate the arrow.** A clear, simple, structural
   arrow with the *+ prāṇa* label.
