# Generativity Analysis

The tracked record contains the source inventories, review decisions, generation scripts, tests, summaries, checksums, and compact result tables required to reproduce the analysis. Very large materialized ledgers and the Rust verifier's `target/` directory remain local and are excluded through `.gitignore`; they are generated artifacts rather than canonical research decisions. This keeps the repository deployable without discarding the local tables needed for the future Word Engine exporter.

This directory separates derived bases from their inflected forms. Twelve bounded passes generated **2,174,721 word-meanings under laukika engine mode**. A domain audit moved 8,660 descendants of eleven bases documented as Vedic-only out of that domain, leaving a bounded laukika subtotal of **2,166,061**. Six Vaidika passes then produced a bounded Vaidika subtotal of **213,398** and a combined subtotal of **2,379,459**. Six nominal and broad तद्धित passes add **2,382,230** word-meanings beyond the seven-row pilot already present. Six conditioned तद्धित passes add another **975** source-example relations, and the first six स्त्रीप्रत्यय passes add **50** source-demonstrated feminine word-meanings. Six नामधातुः passes add **3,176,352** derived verbal meanings. A bounded नञ् pass adds one negative counterpart for each of **3,177,340** current nominal meanings. A suffix-operation pass recovers **24** relations encoded through local helpers or constructed inputs. The plain-source pass then returns 248 active assertions to their governing rules and adds **236** meanings after deduplication and reconciliation. A semantic-class generalization pass adds **1,588,155** comparative and superlative meanings after one overlap. A broader lexical स्त्रीप्रत्यय pass adds **140,566** female-agent meanings after two confirmed overlaps while leaving grammatical agreement outside the count. The first bounded समास pass then adds **113** source-demonstrated compound meanings at one derivational depth. Applying one further established operation to the 96 nominal compounds adds **958** depth-two meanings. The current bounded combined subtotal is **12,846,458**.

The completed twelve-pass समास capacity model starts from **8,083,621 compoundable nominal word-meanings**. Four broad relations produce **196,034,769,247,681 formal depth-one semantic slots**. One established post-compound operation produces **1,960,347,692,476,810 additional formal slots**, and one depth-one compound joined to one original nominal meaning produces **9,508,024,664,524,249,997,406 depth-two slots**. These capacities are not materialized vocabulary, dictionary totals, or additions to the 12,846,458-record lexical subtotal. None of these research results has yet replaced the manuscript's schematic total.

The completed twelve-pass तिङन्त model identifies **4,540,848 लौकिक verbal word-meanings** within the materialized subtotal. Ten लौकिक लकाराः provide **45,408,480 third-person singular citation cells**. Expanding each citation coordinate to three persons and three numbers produces **408,676,320 कर्तरि grammatical cells**. The citation cells are contained within the full matrix, not added to it. परस्मैपदम् and आत्मनेपदम् outputs remain alternatives attached to each cell, and वैदिक लेट् remains outside the लौकिक count. These grammatical capacities are not added to the lexical subtotal or materialized as hundreds of millions of database rows.

The completed twelve-pass नामरूप model reconciles **8,084,287 लौकिक name, object, quality, state, and relation meanings** and separates **7,925 complete unchanging meanings** before inflection. One citation coordinate supplies **8,084,287 citation cells**. Eight relation coordinates across one, two, and more than two supply **194,022,888 grammatical cells**. Gender determines the generated output but contributes no automatic multiplier; agreement-driven additional genders remain separate. The pinned engine regenerates all eight citation samples and all 192 cells in eight complete sample paradigms. These grammatical capacities remain outside the lexical subtotal.

The completed [six-pass synthesis](results/generativity_six_pass_synthesis.md) combines the लौकिक grammatical branches without mixing units. **4,540,848 action meanings** occupy **408,676,320** declared verb cells; **8,084,287 name meanings** occupy **194,022,888** declared relation-number cells; and **7,925 complete unchanging meanings** remain single cells. The result is **602,707,133 semantic grammatical cells**. The **213,398 Vedic meanings** stay separate because Vedic inflection has not been modeled. The [publication cascade](results/publication_capacity_cascade.md) explains the path from 2,634 semantic-atom meanings to the lexical and grammatical totals through familiar examples. The [deployment record](results/generativity_manuscript_proposal.md) preserves the wording deployed in Chapter 0, Chapter 12, and their shared endnote.

We count each distinct word-meaning, not each source entry. One word with two distinct meanings contributes two entries. The same word-meaning recorded in several sources contributes one. Different words with similar meanings remain separate. Inflection is a separate expansion, and coincident spellings do not collapse grammatical cells. Spelling totals in the pilot are diagnostics only.

The semantic sample deliberately keeps the same form under different sense IDs. It also keeps different word formations with similar meanings. The counter uses annotated word-and-sense identities, not spelling or English-gloss deduplication. The author accepted the 28-entry sample on 2026-09-12; generation of its forms does not independently verify every semantic annotation or establish a Sanskrit-wide count.

The full base audit is a separate source-extraction stage. Its provisional assignment count precedes complete lexical review: explicit gloss lists can overlap, and separate source entries can represent the same word and meaning. The first commentary pass resolved 28 of the initial 38 interpretation gaps. The follow-up reviewed the remaining ten: five have interpreted lists, three have sourced partial counts, and two remain unenumerated. This brings the source-assignment count to 2,741, or 2,739 after the two documented repeated-entry reductions. These are not yet final word-meaning totals.

The first identity batch reviews ten groups covering 22 source entries. Four groups support five duplicate reductions, bringing the adjusted subtotal from 2,739 to **2,734**.
