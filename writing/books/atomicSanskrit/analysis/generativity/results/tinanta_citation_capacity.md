# तिङन्त Pass 4: Citation-Form Capacity

Each of **4,540,848** verbal word-meanings receives one third-person singular coordinate in each of ten लौकिक लकाराः.

| Calculation | Semantic grammatical cells |
|---|---:|
| 4,540,848 × 10 | **45,408,480** |

The result counts grammatical functions, not unique spellings. Alternative forms and the two पद series remain attached to a cell rather than multiplying it.
