## R02-01: अय् (ay)

Earlier record: IR210. Entries: `01.0546`: gatO; `01.1031`: gatO.

### Before

The approved correction replaced the spurious yam/cessation assignment at 01.1031 with ay/movement. The inventory already contains ay/movement at 01.0546, with a different marker reading.

### Proposed

Keep the two provisional assignments for now. The accented patha confirms that the corrected listing has a svarita marker, while the earlier entry has an anudatta marker. The commentary on 8.2.19 debates how to explain udayati: one explanation uses a different i atom, another permits the relevant pada behavior. That discussion cannot by itself establish that the two ay listings are one repeated word.

**Count:** 2 -> 2 (+0).

  Earlier ay group carries a different marker condition.

## R02-02: भू (bhū)

Earlier record: IR01. Entries: `01.0001`: sattAyAm; `10.0277`: avakalkane; `10.0382`: prAptO.

### Before

Being, avakalkana, and attainment are counted separately. The earlier follow-up wondered whether the commentary heading bhuvo indicated a different citation from bhu.

### Proposed

Retain the three assignments. Kshiratarangini explicitly calls the heading's form a panchami, an ablative reference to bhu, and connects it with the other bhu entries. There is no reason to invent a different bhuv atom from that heading. For avakalkana it gives a concrete mixing example: bhavayati dadhnaudanam, mixing rice with curds. Other commentaries explain the same heading through thinking, affliction, or other readings; retain those attributions separately.

**Count:** 3 -> 3 (+0).
