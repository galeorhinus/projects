## What Stays Separate

- The two पा (*pā*) entries distinguish drinking from protecting even before inflection.
- One ऋच् (*ṛc*) source entry explicitly lists praise and shine. These remain two readings of the same form.
- करण (*karaṇa*) as an action and as an instrument remain separate meanings. आसन (*āsana*) as sitting and as a seat are likewise listed separately, with their evidence levels shown.
- The यङ् (*yaṅ*) rule distinguishes repetition and intensity. The exact पच् (*pac*) example illustrates repetition; the intensive reading is retained as a rule application for review.
- पुत्रीय (*putrīya*) and पुत्रकाम्य (*putrakāmya*) are different formations with a similar meaning. गोत्व (*gotva*) and गोता (*gotā*) likewise remain separate formations. We are not counting abstract concepts alone.
- Do/make and cook/chef are not automatically split into extra entries just because English supplies two glosses.

## Items Left Open

### van-unspecified-meanings

The source says that there are multiple meanings without enumerating them. Do not assign either one definitive sense or an invented number of senses.

Consult a fuller source before counting its meanings.

### rc-duplicate-local-records

Both local records map by exact citation to one richer entry listing praise and shine. The two meanings are separately represented in the sample; the two source rows must not automatically double each meaning.

Retain both provenance records and review edition alignment before merging lexical records.

Two local records map to one candidate with the gloss adane. The old file has no meanings to prove equivalence.
