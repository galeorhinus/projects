# Six-Pass Compound-Input Operation Stack

This block asks what happens when a source-demonstrated compound becomes the input to one more established word-forming operation.

| Pass | Result |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Classify 96 nominal compound meanings and retain 17 अव्ययीभाव indeclinables as complete words. |
| 2 | Declare 958 depth-two relations under ten already established operations; recursive नञ् remains excluded. |
| 3 | Generate all 958 candidates: 864 through Vidyut and 94 through the source-tested नञ् rules. |
| 4 | Reproduce all 958 output sets in a separate verification pass. |
| 5 | Confirm that none of the source-compound inputs occurred in the earlier broad nominal inventory; preserve 95 repeated-spelling groups as separate meanings where their operations differ. |
| 6 | Add **958 word-meanings**, raising the bounded subtotal to **12,846,458**. |

## Examples

| Compound input | Further result | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| महापुरुष (*mahāpuruṣa*) | महापुरुषत्व (*mahāpuruṣatva*) | the state or defining quality denoted by the compound meaning |
| महापुरुष (*mahāpuruṣa*) | महापुरुषवत् (*mahāpuruṣavat*) | one or that in which the compound referent is present |
| महापुरुष (*mahāpuruṣa*) | अमहापुरुष (*amahāpuruṣa*) | the absence, non-occurrence, or negation of the compound meaning |
| राजपुरुष (*rājapuruṣa*) | राजपुरुषीय (*rājapuruṣīya*) | desire the compound referent in relation to oneself |
| राजपुरुष (*rājapuruṣa*) | राजपुरुषीय (*rājapuruṣīya*) | treat an object as comparable to the compound referent |

## Boundary

The block adds exactly one operation after a source-demonstrated nominal compound and stops at derivational depth two. It does not reopen complete अव्ययीभाव compounds, apply नञ् recursively, generate new compounds from these outputs, or infer arbitrary operation sequences. Finite conjugation and nominal declension remain separate.

The result remains a research subtotal. It has not changed the manuscript, companion, covers, or published endnotes.
