This pass checks explicit prefix conditions in the archived Paninian Dhatupatha witness. It does not use dictionary or corpus attestation as an admission test.

- **Dhatupatha 10.239:** No exclusion. The prefix-conditioned avishkara meaning is already represented in the current base-meaning ledger.
- **Dhatupatha 10.377:** No exclusion. The additional prefix-conditioned lengthening meaning is counted in the prefixed meaning-enrichment pass.

## Prefixed Meaning Enrichment

Two source clauses add **21 word-meanings** to the one-prefix ledger: twenty lengthening meanings from prefixed `tanu` and one longing meaning from `ut + kaṭhi`.

The exclusions concern inheritance of a particular recorded meaning. They do not assert that the engine cannot spell a prefixed form, nor do they reject a combination merely because no dictionary records it.
