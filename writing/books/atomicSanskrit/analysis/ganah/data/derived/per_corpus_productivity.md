## On the Bhagavad Gītā

The Bhagavad Gītā is **not present** in the DCS Mahābhārata dump (the canonical BhG range — MBh 6.23–40 — is absent from the conllu files). To extend this analysis to the Gītā specifically, a separate ingest path is needed:

1. **GRETIL plain text** (`https://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/`) + a lemmatization pass via INRIA Sanskrit Heritage Reader or `sanskrit_parser`.
2. **Vedabase / iskcon-desire-tree word-by-word** data — lemma + grammatical form already tagged; format conversion to CoNLL-U is straightforward.
3. **Custom hand-tagging** of the 700 BhG verses — feasible by one person in a few days; smaller corpus than other DCS texts.

`Aṣṭāvakragīta` (~298 verses) is included above as a **thematic proxy** since DCS has it. It is a different text but shares the *gītā* genre and yields a comparable signature.
