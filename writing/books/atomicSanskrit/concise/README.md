Captions add 352 words across the batch's 21 figure/card placements; assembled notes are not counted here. These are draft-file counts, not PDF page estimates. Keeping the remaining allocations unchanged would project **27,333 main-chapter words**, above the rough 20–25k range. The original 5,650-word allowance for Chapters 0–4 predates the approved restorations; do not compress the restored explanations to force them into that allowance.

**Language rule from the sample review:** state the event or claim directly. Avoid announcing that a language “offers a contrast” or “adds a comparison.” Explain the people, actions, and consequences in ordinary English.
