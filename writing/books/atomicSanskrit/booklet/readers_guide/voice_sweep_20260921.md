# Reader's Guide: Voice Sweep, 21 September 2026

## Governing Rule

Simpler vocabulary, the same argumentative voice as the book. Name the actor, describe the action, and explain its consequence. Do not substitute anonymous accounts or classifications for the people and institutions that construct, teach, and enforce them. Do not add unsupported motives or make coercion the sole explanation for preservation.

## Scope and Verification

Reviewed the full narrative, chapter introductions, all 101 invitations, and cover prose. Revised neutralized agency in the polemical passages; retained the technical explanations, numerical scope, personal-speculation markers, all invitation IDs and destinations, and all ten figures. Four invitation texts changed. The full book manuscript was not edited.

The originals are in [the pre-sweep archive](archive/pre_voice_sweep_20260921/). This comparison is also the Lost and Found record for superseded prose; no historical archive was overwritten. The changes below are replacements, except that the Arabic explanation expands one paragraph into three. No argument was retired.

Twenty source-level tests pass, including source structure, destinations, Devanagari pairing, figures, totals, and a check for the named actors and enforcement mechanisms. PDF-dependent tests were deliberately excluded. No PDFs were rebuilt; pagination and visual checks remain pending an authorized render.
