## What Changed

The revision restores the book's disputed arguments in accessible English. The inside-out architecture remains the organizing idea, and the sound and word examples remain its main demonstration.

1. **An explicit early challenge.** A new section introduces the conventional ancestry and codification account, then states the author's contrary claims about Indian engineering, Vedic calibration, and radiance.
2. **A more consequential contrast between orders.** Government serving a shared standard is distinguished from changing the occupant of an apex. The introduction connects discernment to conduct; the ending explains why accepting the invitation to become ārya entails opposing containment.
3. **The Indian-home case is sharpened.** The sound comparison retains the same language sets and counts. The prose states the challenge posed by Indian languages classified outside Sanskrit's assigned family. The mouth, analysis, and distributed-order argument remains connected.
4. **Pāṇini moves before the two domains.** Tamil and formal Arabic expose the limits of documentation alone. Heroic erasure is explained before the larger system that kept Sanskrit calibrated. Analyst, decoder, and documenter all remain.
5. **Both enemies are connected to the system.** Distributed custody resists capture of one institution. The memory section connects accidental loss, deliberate replacement, and the need to keep principles of civilizational order available for reconstruction.
6. **Concealment and projection are explained together.** The Pāṇini example identifies what is hidden and what is substituted. Svarbhānu's eclipse is explicitly a metaphor. The script discussion follows the current appendix's distinction between signs and the architecture they encode.
7. **The fourth-Abrahamic-religion thesis receives a section.** It explains the book's comparison through authorized interpretation, institutional approval, exclusion, and the direction assigned to history. The criticism concerns a doctrinal formation and its conduct, not every scholar or religious adherent.
8. **Institutional responsibility becomes concrete.** A new section introduces the Boden conversion mandate and Deccan College's 1948 historical-dictionary choice, explains the author's charge, and points to Indian resistance. The value of the dictionary's collection remains distinct from its disputed interpretation.
9. **The PIE accusation is stated and explained.** The dictionary ancestry line and asterisk explain the charge of fraud. The section distinguishes a proposed earlier form from an established ancestor and ends with replacement civilizational memory.
10. **Radiance includes a worked comparison.** The jan/birth family gives readers something specific to examine. The guide distinguishes the book's directional inference from routes supported by records of teachers and translators. Transmission of words and transmission of analytical methods remain separate.
11. **Part II uses actual chapter titles.** Every numbered chapter heading, the Epilogue heading, and the named appendix headings match the current manuscript. Plain-language grouping headings remain guide-specific. Fuller introductions restore the arguments omitted from the earlier summaries.
12. **Cover copy matches the revised content.** The inside-front and back cover mention the institutional criticism and replacement memory alongside engineering and inside-out order.
