# Part IV — The Sun's Atoms

*Particle, atom, molecule, assembly.*

---

The earlier parts cleared the shadow's first blocks, presented Sanskrit's own account, and mapped its sound architecture. Here the fractal claim becomes procedural: the architecture is built in sequence.

No new obstruction is removed in this movement. The already-opened light now shows what the earlier distortions had hidden: the atomic architecture beneath sound.

![Figure E.8 — With three blocks removed, the cleared light reveals the next scale: sound-particles becoming semantic atoms.](figures/eclipse_spine/eclipse_40_part04_sun_atoms_removed_2_3_4.svg){#fig:eclipse-part04-sun-atoms width=100%}

Chapter 10 shows how sonomers enter timed scaffolds and become धातुः (*dhātuḥ*) atoms. It tests those atoms against the six characteristics of a सूत्र (*sūtra*) and finds the same design discipline recurring at several scales.

Chapter 11 follows the atom as it becomes a completed verb. Chapter 12 extends the construction into other words and complete sentences. The Vedic examples demonstrate that this grammar already existed in use. The Vedas preserve completed forms across the construction, while the लौकिक (*laukika*) domain applies the same architecture to new expression. Pāṇini made the recurring operations explicit; he did not create them.

The sequence is particle, atom, molecule, and assembly. Sanskrit builds upward through these scales while the smaller constituents remain recoverable inside each larger form. This is the linguistic fractal in construction form.
