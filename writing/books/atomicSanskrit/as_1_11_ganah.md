*The central architectural claim. Pāṇini decoded one slice of the engineering — the inflectional classification (*gaṇāḥ*), marked by *vikaraṇa*. The deeper engineering classification arranges the 2,168 *dhātavaḥ* on a Mendeleev-style periodic grid: structural axes determine reactivity, semantic regularity emerges from cell position, and a small polyvalent core (Sanskrit's carbon class) generates the vast majority of the derivable lexicon. The empirical work runs the analyses the *vaiyākaraṇāḥ* did not have to run — re-proving in chemistry register what was once the floor of the conversation.*
