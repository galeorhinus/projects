\chapter*{Note to Reviewers}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Note to Reviewers}

Thank you for reading this pre-publication edition of \emph{Atomic Sanskrit}.
There is no expectation that you produce a formal review or finish the entire
manuscript. Comments on a few chapters can be just as useful as a response to
the whole book.

I would especially value knowing what convinces you, what confuses you, where
I assume too much, and where the argument needs stronger evidence or
explanation. Comments in the PDF, messages or voice notes as you read, and
conversations after a few chapters are all welcome.

The private review site and commenting instructions are available at
\url{https://secondshanti.org/as/}. Comments may also be sent directly to
\href{mailto:paragtope@gmail.com}{paragtope@gmail.com}.

Please identify this review version, \textbf{AS-RC-2026-09-01}, and include
the page number when commenting on a specific passage. Pagination may change
before publication.
