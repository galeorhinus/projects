# *Atomic Sanskrit: Source and Reference Companion* — Pre-Publication Back Cover

Did Sanskrit "evolve" until Pāṇini "codified" it? Are the Vedas merely "religious chants"? Did Sanskrit descend from Proto-Indo-European, or do Greek, Latin, and other Eurasian languages preserve reflections of Sanskrit's outward radiance?

*Atomic Sanskrit* takes a clear position. Sanskrit's sounds, atoms, and grammar demonstrate that the language was engineered. The greater feat is the living Vedic system that has kept Sanskrit calibrated against unintended change and deliberate attack for thousands of years. The book also argues that Sanskrit's radiant architecture traveled beyond India.

The *Source and Reference Companion* makes the evidence behind that argument available for inspection.

The main book uses concise numbered endnotes so that its argument can proceed without forcing every reader through the complete apparatus. Each note carries a stable identifier. The same identifier leads to a full entry in this volume containing the primary-source passage, complete citation, translation, verification trail, source history, and an explanation of how the evidence supports the book.

Reference-only appendices preserve the larger datasets, calculations, comparisons, and technical demonstrations.

The main volume develops the case. The companion allows reviewers, critics, Sanskrit scholars, and interested readers to examine how that case was built.
