# Message to the CSU Vice-Chancellor

Draft for the October 7, 2026 meeting at Deccan College.

---

Namaste Professor Srinivas-ji,

I have several ideas for joint research and teaching programmes that Deccan College and CSU could undertake when the time is right.

One that stands out is **Sanskrit as a language system**. We could study its architecture, built on two domains: लौकिक (*laukika*) and वैदिक (*vaidika*). The Vedas serve as what I describe as a "calibrant": a shared standard for checking and correcting language use. How do the two domains work together to keep the language unchanged while allowing millions of new words to be created? This is one of the central arguments in *Atomic Sanskrit*.

We could begin with specific research projects, fellowships, visits by traditional teachers, or a short course. Over time, these could grow into a larger programme or a funded chair.

I’m also exploring potential donors, particularly families with a history of supporting Sanskrit learning, who might support individual projects or fellowships.

Would this be worth discussing at the October 7 meeting? I’d be happy to send a short note outlining a few concrete proposals.
