# Rajiv Malhotra — Outreach Profile

## Relationship

- No prior relationship. Future outreach planned.
- Most aligned Indic-side scholar with *Atomic Sanskrit*'s prosecution of the Western philological orthodoxy — on a different axis (see *How His Work Relates* below).

## Who He Is

- Indian-American author, founder of the **Infinity Foundation** (Princeton, NJ).
- Tech-entrepreneur background (sold his communications-technology company in the mid-1990s), then withdrew from industry to fund and produce a sustained corpus of Indic-civilizational scholarship.
- Operates as a ***non-academic*** scholar — deliberately outside the university tenure track. Funds his own research, commissions others, runs the **Swadeshi Indology** series, and engages public debate directly.
- Polemic voice: prosecutorial, direct, frequently combative with the academic establishment. Comfortable with controversy.

## Core Framework

The standing position across his corpus:

- Indian civilization (***Sanātan*** / Hindu *dharma*) is a coherent, distinct, irreducible civilizational order — not a regional flavor of universal religion, not a power-imposition by an elite, not a defective version of something Western.
- That order has been institutionally suppressed by three successive overlays: colonial Indology; post-colonial Indian "secular" academic frame; contemporary Western religious / orientalist apparatus that absorbs and translates Indic concepts on alien terms.
- The work is to recover institutional ownership of Indic studies: who funds, credentials, employs, publishes, and represents Indic-civilizational scholarship.

## Key Terminology

| Term | What it names |
|---|---|
| ***पूर्वपक्ष (pūrvapakṣa)*** | Classical Hindu debate method: master the opponent's position fully and steelman it before refuting. Methodological commitment in *The Battle for Sanskrit*. |
| ***प्रतिपक्ष (pratipakṣa)*** | Your own counter-position, articulated after the *pūrvapakṣa* has earned the right to it. |
| ***Swadeshi indology*** | Indian-led, *paramparā*-internal Indic studies; institutional counter to the American Orientalist establishment. |
| ***American Orientalism*** / ***Pollockian school*** | Western academic apparatus reading Sanskrit through a power / oppression / political-construction lens, with Sheldon Pollock as figurehead. |
| ***Digestion*** | Pattern by which Western academia absorbs Indic concepts into its own frame, neutralizing their distinctness while crediting itself with the synthesis. *Being Different* develops the term. |
| ***U-turn theory*** | Western scholars take Indic concepts, repackage them in Western frames, then return them to Indians as Western insights the Indians did not previously have. |
| ***Insider / outsider*** binary | Who has standing to study and represent Sanskrit and Indic traditions — a *paramparā*-internal practitioner vs. a Western academic with no living *paramparā* anchor. |

## Method

The ***pūrvapakṣa***-first commitment is the most distinctive methodological feature. His books typically restate the opponent's position at length and in good faith before mounting the prosecution. This is worth matching in any correspondence — meeting him on the method he insists on.

He is also explicit about institutional analysis: not just intellectual positions but funding flows, credentialing pipelines, journal prestige hierarchies, and university hiring patterns. He treats institutional ecology as part of the prosecution, not adjacent to it.

## How His Work Relates to *Atomic Sanskrit*

The two prosecutions run on complementary axes:

- **Malhotra's axis** — *authority and representation*. Who has standing to interpret Sanskrit. Whose frame counts as legitimate scholarship. Who controls the institutional credentialing system that produces "experts."
- **Atomic Sanskrit's axis** — *architecture and engineering*. What Sanskrit *is* (engineered or naturally drifting). What Pāṇini *did* (decoded or codified). What the *paramparā* preserves (the engineered architecture or just the surface).

The two are complementary, not redundant. Malhotra defends *who gets to study Sanskrit*; *Atomic Sanskrit* defends *what Sanskrit is as an engineered object*. Neither prosecution displaces the other; each protects a flank the other cannot reach alone.

In the *Battle Positions* appendix terminology, both prosecutions stand in Position 1 (pre-Pāṇinian engineering inside the *paramparā*'s self-understanding) against Positions 2 (heroic erasure / Pāṇini-as-codifier) and Position 3 (Pollock / sociopolitical construction).

## Potential Outreach Framing

*Atomic Sanskrit* is positioned to complement *Battle for Sanskrit* on the architecture axis where Malhotra's work runs on the authority axis. Possible angles to lead with:

- **The engineering thesis as a structural anchor.** Pre-Pāṇinian engineering — *varṇamālā*, *Dhātupāṭha*, calibration matrix, four preservation modes — as the architectural counterpart to his institutional prosecution. Sanskrit is not just an object of contested authority; it is an engineered object that the contested authority is fighting *over*.
- **The Bṛhaspati keystone (RV 10.71.2).** Bṛhaspati's *vācam akrata* compresses the engineering thesis into one *ṛc* inside the corpus the orthodoxy itself canonizes. A *paramparā*-internal anchor neither the orthodoxy nor Pollock can dislodge. Sits at the heart of the architectural prosecution.
- **The chemistry / fractal-architecture framework.** New vocabulary (*sonomer*, *atomic scaffold*, *bonding chemistry*, *Auditure / Mnemoniture / Flexture / Scripture*) for the Indic-civilizational position. Provides Position-1 advocates fresh terminology that does not depend on either traditional Sanskrit terms (which the orthodoxy translates away) or Western analytical terms (which the orthodoxy already owns).
- ***Pūrvapakṣa* honored in the book's structure.** The book commits substantial space to restating the orthodox account in detail (botanical metaphor, codification claim, PIE, racial Arya thesis, Pollock's *cosmopolis*) before prosecuting each. Method-aligned with his.
